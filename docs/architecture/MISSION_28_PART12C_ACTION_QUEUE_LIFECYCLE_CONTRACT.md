## Exact lifecycle boundary

One lifecycle belongs to one exact tenant-private Part 12A or Part 12B queue-item material identity. Its immutable head pins the queue item, Part 4 snapshot and authoritative head, Part 5 policy/evaluation, lifecycle revision/event digest, group, priority, assignee, due window, unresolved-consequence state and unavailable completion state. The origin queue kind, action family, fixed reason, approval result, assignee role and evidence profile must match the released origin contract. After initial assignment, every non-coalesced operation appends exactly one next revision under the exact current lifecycle head. Its event digest binds the predecessor, operation, target, source/policy, actor/capability, assignment and timing material. Event time, observation time and request arrival never establish order or a second idempotency identity.

The closed operations are initial assignment, exact duplicate coalescing, grouping, priority change, snooze, dismiss, reassignment, escalation, expiry, reopen and source correction. Exact replay returns only immutable no-effect evidence before later mutable currentness is reconsidered, after exact shape and digest verification. A same-ID conflict or a new request, operation, lifecycle or event-ID semantic clone is denied even when observation/evaluation/request clocks change. Dedupe accepts only the complete semantic fingerprint. Grouping keeps every item, lifecycle, source and unresolved-consequence identity visible; different source, policy, horizon (including its exact start), quality, uncertainty, assignee, reason or consequence material can never be collapsed.

Snooze is finite. Dismissed and expired items remain explicitly unresolved and visible. Escalation changes only internal attention priority and never creates legal, financial, recipient, provider or effect authority. Reopen requires exact-current source and policy material and would append a new event only after later runtime authority. Part 3A has no released capability or record-scope assignment for internal-action lifecycle mutation; an owner/admin role never substitutes for that missing capability. Completion remains unavailable in every state.

An exact source correction or tombstone has precedence over an authority-increasing operation. It preserves the old item and all prior events, marks the old item source-changed/stale, and binds the exact same-lineage next snapshot plus authoritative head whose effective snapshot identity and digest equal that replacement. Consequential unresolved work requires a distinct successor material digest; candidate data cannot claim that successor was persisted. Corrections never rewrite history or silently hide work.
