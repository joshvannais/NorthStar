# Mission 26 Part 7A — planned labor-cost prerequisite

The unmounted `src/forecasting/laborCostPositionDiagnostic.js` calculates a bounded **planned labor cost**, not a forecast. It reuses Mission 24's `laborPlanContract.calculate` for worker-hours, burden, per-task cents rounding and unknown-rate handling. A caller supplies one pinned plan per estimate, its persisted currency matching the single reporting currency, and a future work window. A job that straddles the requested period is withheld; it is not assigned wholly to that month or split by an invented allocation rule. Missing rates or burden, unknown/expired cost-source applicability, incomplete or revoked record coverage, and pagination all return unavailable rather than zero. A complete empty claimed set can produce zero **only as a hypothetical position**.

The output always says `sourceAuthenticated: false`, `capacityVerified: false`, `learnedRateApplied: false`, and `forecastIssued: false`. The caller's coverage and revision/digest claims are structurally checked, not certified. An owner-authenticated source adapter must establish current/adopted M24 plan identity, supersession and withdrawal, complete as-of visibility, relevant approved work and scheduling, rate applicability, job allocation across periods, tenant and role access, cancellation/change handling and compatible M25 labor outcomes before a paid forecast or calibrated interval can issue. A rate source labeled as an owner estimate remains that kind of evidence; a date and geography label do not turn it into verified payroll or a supplier quote. A planned cost does not prove that workers are available or qualified.

Part 5B's exact as-of workforce/availability source gate is still open. Mission 25's consented labor calibration is advisory and tenant-private; applying it to hours requires a separately accepted source reader, sample/applicability checks and a policy for avoiding a second adjustment where an M24 plan already reflects the learned rate. No M24/M25 contract, migration, route or historical record is changed here. Part 7A and Part 7 acceptance remain open.

Tests exercise M24 arithmetic reuse, absent rates and burden, source expiry, unknown/cross-period windows, incomplete coverage, wrong tenant, later-record leakage, duplicate identities and bounded inputs. Future acceptance needs an authenticated paid/demo journey, historical cutoff and correction tests in role-separated PostgreSQL, capacity and learned-rate evidence, period attribution, independent exact-head audit and the normal release gate.
