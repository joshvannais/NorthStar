## Server-side authorization contract

Every later Mission 27 read or mutation must resolve the following from server-owned current authority. Omission, ambiguity, conflict, staleness, or caller substitution is a named value-free denial.

1. **Tenant and actor:** current organization, user, membership, access role, action capability grant and grant revision. Every grant is scoped to one exact action phase (`request`, `approve` or `execute`), target class and tenant; no wildcard grant is accepted, and permission to request never implies permission to approve or execute. All referenced records share the exact tenant composite key. A role is context, never the grant.
2. **Session assurance:** current non-revoked session, CSRF for browser mutation, authentication time, authentication method, MFA result when required, and policy-defined recent-auth deadline. Session creation or `last_used_at` is not proof of recent authentication. Current code has no accepted recent-auth/MFA fact, so actions requiring either remain unavailable.
3. **Purpose and target:** exact action name, purpose, target identity, target revision/digest, source identities/revisions/digests, adapter/build/config identity, currency, exact amount components and requested result. A provider identifier or public token never substitutes for these pins.
4. **Currentness and concurrency:** trusted-clock evaluation, source state, optimistic revision, idempotency key, request digest, expiry and transaction fences are checked again in the effect transaction. Exact replay returns the original receipt; changed reuse conflicts; in-flight duplicates serialize; an uncertain result is recovered by receipt lookup rather than repetition.
5. **Human control:** requester, reviewer and executor identities are distinct where the matrix requires independent approval. A browser assertion, caller-supplied approver, delegated provider account, automation or Mission 28 future policy cannot satisfy the requirement.
6. **Bounded amount:** any money-bearing action must use one explicit ISO currency and exact minor units or canonical decimal. Its accepted amount is non-negative, within the action/currency policy bound, equal to current source arithmetic and covered by the same approval. A missing numeric policy bound denies the action. Slice 1D will choose launch values; until then no money-bearing capability is available.
7. **Immutable reason and receipt:** consequential mutations require a bounded nonblank human reason selected or written at the point of action. It cannot be silently defaulted, inherited from another action or reused across request digests. The append-only receipt records action, purpose, tenant, actor, session assurance reference, capability/grant revision, requester/approver/executor identities, reason, policy/version, target and source pins, exact money/currency when applicable, idempotency/request digest, trusted time, outcome, effect identity and before/after digests. It excludes secrets, raw payment instruments, private transcripts, source images and unnecessary customer content.
8. **Direct-table denial:** the runtime role cannot bypass the public service/procedure. Database-side authority repeats tenant, actor, session, grant, currentness, amount, approval and replay checks immediately before mutation; tables are not writable directly by the app role.

## Assurance and control levels

| Level | Required proof | Self-approval and dual control | Current availability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `current_session` | Current tenant session, active membership/subscription as applicable, explicit capability, target/source pins and CSRF for browser mutation | One authorized actor may perform a reversible draft/review action; immutable actor/reason/revision still applies | Capability grants do not exist, so unavailable |
| `recent_auth` | Everything above plus server-owned authentication time within the action policy window | Requester may execute only when the matrix says `single_control`; an action marked `conditional_dual_control` requires a different current approver when its policy trigger is met | Recent-auth fact/window do not exist, so unavailable |
| `recent_auth_mfa` | Everything above plus an accepted MFA ceremony bound to actor, session, purpose and trusted time | `dual_control` always requires a different eligible approver; no actor may approve their own request or reuse one approval for another request/digest | MFA and independent-approval authority do not exist, so unavailable |
| `scoped_customer_session` | High-entropy, hashed, expiring, revocable, rate-limited token/session bound to one exact document/action, recipient policy and current server-side target | Customer can perform only the exact permitted view, correction request, signature evidence or hosted-payment initiation; it never grants staff capability or provider-side authority | No Mission 27 customer session exists, so unavailable |
| `authenticated_provider_event` | Verified signature against server-selected endpoint secret/account/mode, bounded clock tolerance, unique provider event/object identity, current adapter version, tenant/account binding and fetch/reconciliation when required | Contributes evidence only. It never approves a new charge, refund, write-off, invoice, edit, conversion or communication | No accepted Mission 27 provider exists, so unavailable |

`single_control` never means role-only or unchecked self-approval. For `conditional_dual_control`, one current human may request and execute only when Slice 1D explicitly supports that single-control path, the exact amount is at or below its action/currency ceiling, every other policy condition is satisfied, and the actor supplies a fresh confirmation and reason. The actor cannot manufacture a second approval. When any policy trigger requires dual control, two different current humans with compatible capabilities, independent recent-auth/MFA proof where required, one exact request digest and one unexpired approval are mandatory; missing bounds or conditions deny the action. `dual_control` always requires those two humans. There is no emergency, owner, administrator, provider, support or direct-database bypass. A solo tenant that cannot supply a required independent approver receives `dual_control_unavailable` and no effect.

## Denial and currentness rules

- The first failed prerequisite produces a stable named denial or value-free unavailable state; no amount, recipient, provider state, approval, total, token or action preview survives it.
- Newest evidence that is malformed, unsupported, unauthorized or stale blocks issuance/action. The system does not silently select an older convenient source.
- Exact zero remains a valid amount only when source, currency, calculation and policy prove it. Zero is never a default for absent, unknown, stale, conflicting or unsupported evidence.
- Revoked membership/session/grant, expired recent-auth/MFA, changed role, changed policy/limit, changed source/target digest, changed amount/currency, changed recipient/provider account/mode, changed adapter version, new legal hold/dispute, or a required approver becoming ineligible invalidates authorization before effect and before exact replay is returned.
- Provider/customer acknowledgement is evidence of its own act only. A success callback cannot supply missing business authority or conceal an uncertain outcome.
