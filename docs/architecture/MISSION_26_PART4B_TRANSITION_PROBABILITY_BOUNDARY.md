# Mission 26 Part 4B — lead and booking transition boundary

Status: bounded internal candidate, not an accepted probability forecast. NorthStar has not operated a public Retell source long enough to establish complete, comparable lead history. Attorney review remains a separate prerequisite to public calling. This expected lack of live history does not require recovery of old setup calls and does not prevent synthetic validation or independent work in later Mission 26 slices. Paid predictions remain unavailable until their own source and evaluation gates pass.

Part 3A defines four different as-of cohort transitions: qualification, accepted estimate request, accepted booking, and booking cancellation. A candidate cohort is frozen at its prediction cutoff. Only its first qualifying transition strictly after that cutoff and inside the stated horizon can enter its numerator. A new lead or appointment created after the cutoff cannot enter the denominator. A reschedule is not automatically a cancellation; a preliminary estimate is not an accepted estimate request; a desired appointment is not an accepted booking. Each target requires a separate versioned owner-source reader and later finalized outcome reader. No current caller-supplied summary proves those facts.

`src/forecasting/transitionRateDiagnostic.js` performs one narrow calculation over supplied fictional or future guarded finalized cohort summaries. It validates tenant-wide reporting windows, cutoffs, mutually distinct period starts, nonzero eligible denominators, complete outcomes, units of count, matching prediction lead time, and same-context comparability. It pools transition counts over eligible counts, so a tiny cohort does not receive the same weight as a large one. Missing, revoked, incomparable, zero-denominator or absent cohorts return a named unavailable state. The result is explicitly `descriptive_only`, `sourceAuthenticated: false`, `confidence: unavailable`, and `forecastIssued: false`. It has no route, saved run or UI and grants no access to a tenant record. Tests use fictional cohorts; they prove arithmetic and refusal behavior, not a business's transition probability.

The next authorized source step for each target is a purpose-bound, tenant-isolated as-of cohort receipt from the owning lead or scheduling authority. It must pin the exact identities at the cutoff without leaking later facts, then a separate outcome receipt must prove first transition time, correction history, consent/retention and complete finality. Part 3B must compare saved predictions with later outcomes, Part 3C must establish target-specific sufficiency/calibration policy from real evidence, Part 3D must review the executable algorithm, and Part 11 must preserve immutable runs. Until then, a historical observed fraction is neither a calibrated probability nor a production forecast.

Part 4A's Retell call-source work remains separate. A caller-reported request, distinct lead, qualified lead, estimate request and accepted booking are not interchangeable. Part 4B can be developed with synthetic records while live Part 4A coverage remains unavailable, but it cannot borrow a fictional cohort or an old setup call to issue a paid prediction. Part 4D must show a clear unavailable state for paid tenants lacking verified history and label all demo evidence as fictional. No provider action, public calling, legal wording or commercial automation is authorized by this candidate.
