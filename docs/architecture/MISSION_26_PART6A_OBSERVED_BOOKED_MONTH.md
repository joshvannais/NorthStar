# Mission 26 Part 6A: observed current booked-job cohort by confirmation month

This local candidate groups up to 100 owner-confirmed booked jobs by the UTC month in which their confirmation was recorded. The guarded paid owner/admin read checks every included confirmation's current accepted estimate, scheduled appointment, commercial review and exact before-tax price before adding it. It returns an exact observed count, amount and currency only when every record in the bounded cohort remains current and all use one currency. A changed confirmation, oversized cohort, mixed currency or empty month returns unavailable without a numeric amount. Other tenants and member roles cannot see the data.

The result is **jobs whose current confirmation was recorded in that UTC month**, read under a tenant source lock. It does not establish when each job was first booked. It is not a month-end snapshot or proof of every booked job in the business. `completePeriodVerified` and `wholeBusinessCoverageVerified` remain false even on the positive path. Historical month-end states, off-platform jobs, earned revenue and collected cash are outside this observation. A later correction makes the earlier confirmation unavailable rather than retaining its prior monthly value. No forecast or full Part 6A baseline is issued.

The mounted fictional-tenant test proves one positive observed month, empty history unavailable, tenant/role denial, and a later review correction suppressing the amount. Complete source-ordered monthly coverage, paid/demo presentation, cross-source contention and full-slice acceptance remain open.
