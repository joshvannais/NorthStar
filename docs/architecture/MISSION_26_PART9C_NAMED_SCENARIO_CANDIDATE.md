# Mission 26 Part 9C - authenticated named pipeline scenarios

Migration 250 and the mounted `/api/v1/forecast/named-pipeline-scenarios` boundary complete original Part 9C over Part 6B's source-authenticated open-pipeline position. An owner or admin approves an exact complete set of estimate-specific adverse, base and favorable conversion-weight assumptions. The server authenticates the actor, membership, tenant, current Business Profile, pipeline policy, preliminary or approved-but-unbooked estimate identity, current revision and price lineage. It generates the source kind, digest, record time, applicability and review revision rather than trusting caller-supplied provenance.

The reader replays Part 6B's exact cents-times-millionths arithmetic with one rounding after the complete bounded sum. Input order is normalized by estimate identity. The base weight must equal the current reviewed Part 6B central policy for that estimate category, and adverse must be no greater than base, which must be no greater than favorable. Changing an assumption, source member, estimate revision, profile, policy or review changes the corresponding digest or makes the old result unavailable. Authenticated complete zero remains a numeric zero; absent or incomplete authority remains value-free unavailable.

The labels remain **alternative assumptions for one future open-pipeline value**. They are not historical facts, probabilities, confidence, P10/P50/P90, earned revenue, collected cash or calibrated ranges. "Favorable" is the highest assumed open-pipeline value and is not a favorable-cost case. Booked work remains excluded. Blanket whole-estimate percentages, incomplete member coverage, an incorrect base, crossed scenario order and unauthenticated member identities are rejected.

The append-only review table is purpose-fixed to Part 9C assumptions and currentness. It does not implement Part 11's general forecast-run platform. Reviews may be replaced or revoked through optimistic concurrency and idempotent guarded entry points. Reads fail closed and clear every issued value after review revocation, clock reversal, elapsed horizon, Business Profile change, pipeline-policy change or source change. The boundary is research-only and authorizes no price change, booking, scheduling, recommendation or automatic action. Part 9D sensitivity and Part 9E paid/demo presentation remain separate.

The earlier `namedPipelineScenarioCandidate` stays as a pure unmounted prerequisite and regression target. Mounted acceptance is proven on fictional data through strict HTTP-contract tests and a fresh disposable PostgreSQL chain through migration 250, including tenant and role isolation, exact provenance, input-order replay, zero versus absent, policy currentness, clock reversal, review revocation and runtime privilege reconciliation.
