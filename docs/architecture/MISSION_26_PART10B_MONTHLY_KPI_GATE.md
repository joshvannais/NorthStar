# Mission 26 Part 10B — monthly KPI coherence gate

Status: architecture-only prerequisite. No monthly forecast KPI number, chart, run, or source reader is issued. Final Part 10B acceptance remains open.

Part 10B calls for monthly revenue, operating cost, profit, margin, demand and capacity cards and graphs **from the same forecast run**. Today's `m26-forecast-output-v1` describes one target and one horizon; Part 3B's supplied comparison receipts and Part 10A's projection cannot prove a complete, authorized multi-target run. Mission 26 Part 11B has not yet supplied the immutable run inventory and bundle identity needed to claim that six separately produced values belong to one run. A matching month or snapshot digest alone is insufficient: the values could use different cutoffs, target versions, corrections, policies or source populations.

## Required display relationship

An eventual saved run must own a bounded manifest of target outputs for one tenant and prediction cutoff. Each card and each graph point must reference that run identity, its versioned target, exact half-open monthly reporting window, business time zone and calendar basis, unit/currency, calculation version, source snapshot and coverage receipt, current correction status, and applicability. The manifest must identify absent targets explicitly. A chart may contain multiple months, but each point must retain its own run identity; a current-versus-prior comparison uses Part 10A's separate comparability gate. A card must not quietly select a newer revenue output and an older cost output to manufacture profit.

The eventual bundle validator must reject tenant, horizon, currency, calendar, target-version, applicability or run-identity mismatch. An invalid or missing component withholds dependent profit/margin while unrelated valid slots may remain independently available **only** when the owning run and UI explain that partial coverage. Forecast ranges retain Part 9's deterministic-versus-calibrated distinction. Source failure, correction, revocation or deletion must flow through Part 11C invalidation and visibly retire affected card and chart points without rewriting historical runs.

## Current safe experience and acceptance

The paid and synthetic demo Command Center may explain that the monthly forecast is unavailable until the required evidence and run exist. It must not render zero placeholders, fabricated graphs, default profit, or a generic confidence percentage. A static live dashboard KPI is not a forecast merely because its label says “forecast.” The earlier Part 6D revenue-wording boundary and Part 7E missing-overhead gate apply here.

Final acceptance requires an authenticated same-run manifest and source readers; source-complete positive and observed-zero months; partial and conflicting coverage; one long job allocated over multiple months; corrected/revoked records; incompatible currency/calendar/cutoff/target versions; double-cost prevention; zero revenue; role-specific capacity; paid/demo isolation; current-versus-prior comparisons; rendered plain-language desktop/mobile cards, graph, empty/error/recovery states; and an independent exact-head audit. CI, provider, private-production and physical-device evidence remain separately reported as available or unavailable. This document does not reopen released Mission 24 or Mission 25 contracts and does not invent a recognition or aggregation policy.
