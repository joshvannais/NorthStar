## Evidence remains evidence

The six evidence classes stay separate. An attempt does not establish sent or delivered. Accepted, sent, delivered, failed and unknown delivery labels do not establish a reply. A reply, question or opt-out does not establish a payment or promise. A reviewed promise is not payment authorization, settlement or collection. Suppression/rebuild and owner-review labels do not establish a communication, task, legal action or financial outcome. No class may be strengthened by another class, a missing value, array order, time order, alias, clone or replay.

The released Mission 27 Part 10 acceptance remains `unavailable` with reason `part10_collection_evidence_unavailable`. It supplies no exact current collection-outcome identity. Therefore the current Part 11F result is the fixed, privacy-safe state **Collection reconciliation unavailable — exact current outcome evidence is not available**. Payment, receipt, settlement, offline-tender, accounting, balance or zero-balance evidence cannot be relabeled as a Mission 27 collection outcome. Only a later separately accepted exact-current Mission 27 collection-outcome authority could supply that fact.
