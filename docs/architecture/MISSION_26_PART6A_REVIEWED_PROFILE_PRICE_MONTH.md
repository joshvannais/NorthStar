# Mission 26 Parts 6A/2B: reviewed-profile local price-month candidate

Status: isolated, unreleased bounded integration candidate. Neither full slice is complete.

The paid owner/admin `GET /api/v1/forecast/price-history/ordered-snapshots/:snapshotId/reviewed-profile-month-candidate` path joins an immutable ordered Mission 24 approved-price receipt to one completed local month whose Business Profile applicability an owner or administrator explicitly reviewed. A guarded database read serializes with attestation confirmation/revocation for that tenant-month and locks the pinned Business Profile row through the read transaction. The existing ordered-price read then holds its source-order lock until commit. The route recomputes the normalized profile hash before deriving the local month. The private raw profile, private raw-profile digest, individual decisions and global source-order identifiers never enter the API response.

An absent, revoked or changed profile claim yields an unavailable result with no window. A surviving claim permits a pinned local-month calculation, but `historicalCalendarVerified`, `observationCoverageVerified`, `sourceMonthVerified`, `calendarPeriodVerified`, `eligibleForForecast`, `wholeBusinessCoverageVerified` and `forecastIssued` remain false. The claim cannot turn a pre-anchor empty month into zero approved prices. It does not establish off-platform completeness, booked work, earned revenue, customer payment, comparable periods, statistical sufficiency or a future approved-price baseline. The existing current-profile and UTC-month routes remain unchanged.

Mounted disposable-PostgreSQL tests use fictional tenants and a real ordered receipt to cover missing, confirmed, changed and revoked claims; a held claim read that makes a concurrent revocation fail busy; a pre-anchor unavailable month; tenant/role/request-shape gates; response minimization and no forecast promotion. **They do not demonstrate a positive completed month after the ordered source anchor or a non-null approved-price amount through this route.** That mounted positive-path gate remains open because the source-owned anchor is current; the fixture does not backdate it. Separate pure arithmetic tests do not substitute for this integration proof. Full Part 6A/2B acceptance still requires that positive path, verified historical calendar authority, complete source observations, a forecast algorithm/run/evaluation path and later live-data validation. CI, private production, provider, physical-device and real tenant-history evidence are not claimed.
