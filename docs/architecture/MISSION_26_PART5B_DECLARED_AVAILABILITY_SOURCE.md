# Mission 26 Part 5B — guarded declared-availability source

Status: internal, unmounted read-only source prerequisite. This is **not** an issued capacity forecast or final Part 5B acceptance. No route, migration, schedule action, assignment, reservation, or customer-facing value is added.

`readDeclaredAvailabilitySnapshot` reuses Mission 22's organization, current actor/session/subscription/onboarding, and active Business Profile checks inside one repeatable-read transaction. It reads the active tenant roster and the existing canonical declared-availability authorities and intervals through Mission 22's bounded attachment reader. It also reads current scheduled assignments through Mission 22's bounded schedule reader, retaining exact assignment revisions, digests, overlapping intervals and currently attached worker identities. A complete source snapshot requires a current Business Profile hours object, at most 100 active workers, untruncated skill, availability and schedule evidence, declared availability coverage for every active worker across the requested future window, and no unapproved, unassigned or inactive-worker schedule in that window. A missing or bounded source returns unavailable, never zero. The snapshot preserves profile, time-zone, interval revision/digest, worker identity, service associations, the database MVCC snapshot identity, a microsecond-precision observation time recorded after the snapshot was established, and one digest for later source-currentness checks. The observation time is an **upper bound on the database read**, not a certified historical as-of cutoff; `temporalCutoffVerified` remains false. It is internal evidence only and carries private workforce information.

The snapshot authenticates **these current source records**, not a worker's qualification for a particular job or exact cutoff eligibility. A workforce operational role or service association is not proof of a certification or safe assignment. `approvedScheduleIntervalsRead` means only that the bounded current approved schedule intervals were read; `commitmentsCovered` remains false because this is not complete leave, off-calendar, travel-buffer, crew-membership-at-approval or external-commitment evidence. The reader does not establish actual leave, travel, crew composition feasibility, location reachability, vehicle or equipment availability, operator eligibility, realized attendance, or future capacity. The unmounted role-time diagnostic cannot consume this snapshot as an issued forecast. A later Mission 26 adapter must reconcile the exact role definition, Business Profile working-hour windows and local-time transitions, qualifications, approved M22 commitments, absences, resource constraints and same-cutoff coverage before calculating a constrained capacity position. Any promoted forecast also needs the Part 3 target/output, saved-run/outcome, and evaluation gates. Mission 22 retains assignment authority.

The same snapshot now also reads each roster member's latest append-only Mission 23 work-profile review in the same database snapshot. It records review status, revision, and certification IDs selected at approval with their expiry dates; a missing profile stays missing, and inconsistent records fail closed. These are **approval-recorded certifications**, not proof that a certification remains current throughout the future horizon, maps to a specific job, or grants safe equipment operation. `reviewedWorkProfilesRead` means the bounded source was read; `roleQualificationVerified` remains false. No profile documents or employee claims become customer-facing.

Focused mock-database tests verify that Mission 22's guard is invoked, snapshot fields remain immutable, missing availability, unapproved/unassigned/inactive-worker schedules and bounded roster or schedule evidence withhold data, an inactive actor is refused, malformed requests never reach storage, and failures roll back. They do not claim disposable-PostgreSQL integration, paid/demo UI, CI, private-production source contents, or final capacity acceptance.
