# Mission 26 Part 7D - overhead and financed-asset cash forecast

Migration 243 closes the previously documented source-authority gap with the smallest additive owner-recorded source. `canonical_operating_cost_schedule_revisions` is an append-only, tenant-private complete schedule snapshot owned by Business Profile and asset operations. Mission 26 cannot write it through a forecast route. An owner or administrator privately reads, records, corrects or revokes the source through the guarded Business Profile schedule endpoint; mutations require CSRF, idempotency, expected revision/digest and an exact allocation policy. Authenticated owner/admin current and as-of reads preserve each immutable revision. `GET /api/v1/business-profile/operating-cost-schedules/latest-recorded` separately returns the exact latest recorded predecessor identity; a future-effective replacement is labeled `pending` and is not the active forecast source. This lets an authorized owner/admin correct or revoke pending authority after reload without weakening effective-date selection. The runtime role has no table privileges and can use only the seven guarded mutation/reference/source/forecast entries.

Each active snapshot pins one currency, effective date, inclusive coverage dates, recorded-through instant, complete source-coverage attestation and current revision. It also pins the exact active Business Profile version/hash/time zone/currency, every referenced asset version/state, and a server-derived digest of all current saved Mission 24 equipment-cost and overhead plan references. Every schedule has a stable source key, exact positive constant amount rule, explicit due dates, recurrence end, payment status, included categories and either an owner attestation or a source-document digest. A financed-asset obligation must reference one current tenant asset and explicitly include debt service. Overhead schedules cannot disguise principal, interest or debt service as overhead. Corrections replace the complete set; revocation makes the current forecast unavailable without rewriting earlier as-of results. `effectiveOn` is authoritative: a replacement recorded early remains dormant until its tenant-local effective date, for both current and exact-cutoff source/forecast reads. Revocation takes effect when recorded. A complete attested empty source is the only way zero can issue. Any nonempty source with a zero amount or without positive scheduled commitments in both returned categories fails closed as `nonempty_zero_schedule_unavailable`.

The policy keeps Mission 24 and Mission 26 meanings separate. Mission 24's job-specific overhead allocation, equipment economic recovery and financing-cash allocation remain estimate economics. The server enumerates exact latest saved plan IDs, revisions, digests, methods, category references and overlap results; the owner must reconcile every item against a schedule or record that it is a different obligation. Caller-supplied labels cannot create a successful reconciliation. The Part 7D read never adds a Mission 24 pool, assumes an installment from asset ownership, or treats economic depreciation as cash. It sums only due dates marked `scheduled` inside 30 tenant-local calendar dates. Exact cents arithmetic and mutation validation cap every rolling 30-local-date category aggregate at `999999999999.99`; a legacy source beyond that bound fails closed as `schedule_amount_capacity_unavailable` rather than becoming a request-invalid read. `owner_marked_satisfied` and canceled dates are excluded from future commitments, but the output explicitly says a schedule is not proof that payment occurred.

`GET /api/v1/forecast/overhead-cash/current` and its exact-cutoff `/as-of` counterpart are private owner/admin, no-store, read-only projections. The horizon is explicitly `local_calendar_days` with inclusive `startsOn` and exclusive `endsOnExclusive`; it does not claim a fixed number of elapsed seconds across daylight-saving changes or non-midnight cutoffs. Current reads hold the same organization lock used by Business Profile and asset mutations, plus exact profile/asset row locks and Mission 24 table locks, through projection return. A concurrent mutation cannot leave `currentRevision=true` after its source changed. Current reads fail closed when the complete owner-recorded source is absent/revoked/stale, coverage does not span the local horizon, a source pin changes, or reconciliation is no longer current. Historical reads select the highest immutable revision both recorded by the requested cutoff and effective on its tenant-local date; later corrections, payoff markings, cancellations and revocations cannot rewrite earlier cutoffs. The aggregate response exposes no source keys, asset IDs, documents or Mission 24 identities.

Mission 25 outcome learning does not rewrite contractual due dates or amounts. No learned adjustment is applied. External financial observations remain retrospective context and cannot supply a forward obligation schedule. Complete schedule-source coverage is explicitly narrower than whole-business coverage: `wholeBusinessCoverageVerified` and `offPlatformCoverageVerified` remain false. The forecast also does not claim actual payment, recognized expense, economic depreciation, a complete operating-cost forecast, probability, calibrated range or an automatic action.
