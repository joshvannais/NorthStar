What is NorthStar?

NorthStar is an AI Office Manager and business operating system for home-service businesses. It connects calls, leads, customers, work, estimates, schedules, next actions, and outcomes in one role-authorized operating view.

What can I explore without an account?

The Command Center demo is an isolated workspace. Its mounted destinations share one durable demo session, while configuration destinations remain truthful, stable, and read-only.

Does the Homepage call my phone?

No. The designed experience is a visitor-initiated browser Web Call, not an outbound callback. It requests no phone number. Production activation remains unavailable until final attorney wording, Retell privacy settings, temporary-processing isolation, and verified deletion are independently approved.

What happens to a browser Web Call?

When approved and enabled, the page requires a checked microphone/temporary-processing notice, plays an audible AI-demo and temporary-recording disclosure, and requires affirmative verbal consent. Hang-up or withdrawal begins Retell Delete Call verification and NorthStar purge. Polaris results remain hidden if deletion cannot be verified, and any visible result stays in browser memory only.

Are the Polaris results a quote?

No. The public result uses a versioned demo Business Profile and mounted Polaris intelligence. It demonstrates how supported job facts can become an estimate range and next action; it is not a contractor quote or a promise of availability.

What are the published prices?

The published monthly plans are Starter at $149 with 160 billed call minutes, Growth at $299 with 325 billed call minutes, and Complete at $499 with 540 billed call minutes. Each includes the connected operating workspace and explainable Polaris intelligence described on the pricing page. Provider activation, taxes, discounts, and any authorized overage terms are confirmed before purchase. Enterprise remains an unpriced, tailored review for larger-team, higher-volume, multi-location, or custom operating needs.

What do NorthStar readiness labels mean?

Available now is usable in the stated environment. Available in the account-free demo is isolated demonstration behavior. Requires setup needs tenant configuration. Coming soon is planned but unavailable. Awaiting approval remains disabled until the named provider or legal gate is complete.

Are listed integrations connected?

Not by appearing in the catalogue. Each entry states whether provider approval or future work is required. A catalogue entry does not establish authorization, synchronization, tenant readiness, or live provider configuration.

How do map preferences work?

Authorized users can choose Apple Maps, Google Maps, or Waze as a navigation preference. NorthStar launches the selected provider with the available destination; it does not claim the provider accepted, routed, or completed the trip.

How do I get support?

Signed-in customers can use Report a Bug to receive a durable case reference and organization history. For product or account questions, email Support@northstar-os.ai or use the contact page; a general email draft is not a received case until your provider sends it.

What information is needed to start?

Account creation asks for business name, your name, email, and password confirmation. Business phone, service area, and operating details are collected later during setup. Prospects can use the account-free demo without creating an account.

Does the demo use real customer information?

No. The account-free workspace uses isolated demonstration data. Do not enter real customer details, credentials, payment information, medical information, or other sensitive data into public demonstration inputs.

Does NorthStar act without human approval?

NorthStar separates recommendations and previews from consequential actions. Current scheduling and provider-dependent actions require the applicable account authority, setup, and explicit confirmation. See the Privacy Policy and Terms for the governing public statements.

How is accessibility handled?

NorthStar public pages provide visible labels, keyboard-operable controls, focus indicators, semantic headings, light and dark themes, and responsive layouts. Browser testing does not replace acceptance on every physical device or assistive-technology combination; accessibility problems can be reported through Support.
