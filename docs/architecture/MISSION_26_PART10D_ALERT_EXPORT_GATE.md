## Alert and next-move contract

An eventual alert needs a versioned trigger policy, exact target and reporting window, accepted run/revision, source-currentness decision, applicable baseline or threshold, materiality, direction, uncertainty, explanation and owner-visible time. A threshold crossing caused by stale, revoked, corrected or incomplete evidence must become unavailable or require review, not a confident alert. Avoid duplicate alerts for the same run/policy/event, and distinguish a changed run from a repeated page load. An alert's label must say whether it is a deterministic scenario, calibrated forecast or observed condition. No percentage likelihood appears without Part 3C/9 calibration.

A recommended next move is an **advisory proposal** with the evidence that makes it relevant, expected tradeoff, missing information and receiving workflow. Opening a record is navigation, not approval. Any decision to change a schedule, staff, equipment, estimate, price, billing item or customer message stays with the owning mission; Part 11D later owns the reviewed handoff. Mission 26 cannot silently reserve resources, dispatch workers, alter commercial policy or send communications. The recipient must recheck current authority and exact revisions before applying anything. Owner/administrator visibility follows Part 1C's default; a narrower role projection needs its own accepted authority.

## Export boundary

An eventual export is a **point-in-time evidence packet**, not a promise that old advice remains current. It should identify the tenant, authorized viewer and export time; run/revision and output digests; target/version, unit and horizon; reporting-window and business-calendar basis; source snapshot/as-of and coverage; algorithm/calculation and scenario or interval basis; uncertainty; alert-policy version and reason; current correction/invalidation status; and explicit missing evidence. It must separate supplied prediction, later authenticated actual, calculated difference and human action. The packet must not include raw transcripts, customer contact details, wages, secrets or unrelated records. Part 1C already owns permission, source-validity recheck, private/no-store response, safe audit and paid/demo isolation; Part 10D must consume those controls instead of duplicating them. Demo exports, if later enabled, must be marked fictional and cannot include paid tenant evidence.

Export of a superseded run may be allowed only through separately authorized historical access with a visible superseded/stale label and lawful retention. Revocation or deletion can remove current access to source-derived detail without rewriting an immutable run receipt. A link, download or share target must not be exposed before the route can enforce those boundaries. Mission 28 owns any later approved outbound communication; this slice does not send alerts to customers or staff.

## Acceptance boundary

Final acceptance needs a source-authenticated, owner-authorized saved run and current lineage; a versioned alert policy; stable deduplication; positive, negative, stale, revoked and corrected trigger tests; transparent recommended actions and review-only handoffs; a minimized export with authorization/no-store/readback tests; paid/demo separation, reset and expiry; responsive rendered cards and clear empty/error/recovery wording; and an independent exact-head review. No current provider, CI, private-production, physical-device or founder visual evidence is inferred from this architecture document. Until the prerequisites exist, the existing unavailable panels remain the truthful UI state.
