# Mission 26 Part 8B — claimed asset-meter position prerequisite

The unmounted `src/forecasting/assetMeterPositionDiagnostic.js` adds caller-supplied planned operating hours to one caller-supplied asset's matching hours-meter reading. It compares the resulting reading with a supplied **absolute meter threshold**, not a recurring interval length, using fixed-point thousandths that match Mission 20's recorded meter precision. It refuses mixed units, cross-tenant assets, duplicate use identities, malformed source pins, impossible hours within a work window, overlapping asset uses, incomplete coverage, a meter reset or a reading older than the claimed as-of snapshot. Multiple non-overlapping uses may belong to one job. A threshold crossing is a conditional calculation from the submitted plan, not a verified service date or downtime forecast.

Mission 20 owns the asset and meter event history; Mission 23 owns actual job execution; Mission 24 owns planned equipment, readiness and cost composition; Mission 25 owns reviewed utilization outcomes. Checkout duration is not engine-on or productive operating time. None of those owning sources is authenticated by this pure function. The as-of source digest, complete coverage, operating hours and threshold remain caller claims. The output therefore always marks source authentication, current meter verification, complete plan coverage, maintenance-due verification and forecast issuance false. It returns no threshold-crossing date or downtime risk and creates no maintenance action, assignment, reservation or cost.

Final Part 8B acceptance needs tenant/role-guarded as-of asset and meter history including resets and corrections; an owner-approved threshold or maintenance policy with valid units and applicability; complete non-overlapping planned utilization across approved jobs; evidence of actual operating hours where available; provider-neutral rental/leased asset semantics; calibration against trustworthy outcomes; and paid/demo recovery and independent end-to-end validation. This prerequisite introduces no route, migration or UI, and does not complete Part 8B.
