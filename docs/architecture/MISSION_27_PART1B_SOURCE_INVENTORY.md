## Controlling authority and inventory rule

This inventory implements original Slice 1B and additive gate Q1 of the [frozen Mission 27 roadmap](../roadmap/MISSION_27_CUSTOMER_FINANCIAL_LIFECYCLE.md). The released [Slice 1A contract](MISSION_27_PART1A_AUTHORITY_CONTRACT.md) remains the vocabulary and ownership authority. Mission 24 still owns estimates, accepted commercial decisions, customer-safe proposals, and customer acceptance; Mission 22 owns scheduling and dispatch; Mission 23 owns work evidence; Mission 25 owns tenant-private learning; Mission 26 owns advice; Mission 32 later owns deeper field calculations through the shared adapter seam.

Every current route, table, event, KPI, simulation, provider integration, customer link, subscription/account-billing surface, and quoted-job input family found by the repository inventory is assigned one primary class:

- **authoritative** means accepted authority for the named owning mission only. There is no authoritative Mission 27 customer-financial source at this base.
- **upstream evidence** means an accepted source Mission 27 may consume only after the later owning slice validates exact identity, revision, digest, currentness, purpose, capability, and launch-matrix eligibility.
- **demo-only** means fictional, account-free, resettable evidence that can never cross into a paid tenant, provider, customer link, or financial record.
- **legacy** means retired, unmounted, compatibility-only, browser/file-era, or semantically unsafe code and data that cannot be promoted into Mission 27 authority.
- **unavailable** means no accepted source or capability exists. Missing authority stays named and value-free.

An `authoritative` or `upstream evidence` classification never means “invoiceable,” “paid,” “settled,” “earned,” or “recognized.” The later Mission 27 slice named in the roadmap must still create and accept that exact boundary.
