## Exact calculation

`estimate-commercial-terms-v1` supports USD/CAD/EUR cents without exchange conversion. It starts only from non-included saved pricing lines. Ordered line discounts, one order discount, signed fixed adjustments and explicitly explained fees conserve amounts. Percentage calculations use exact integers and half-up cents; proportional allocation distributes remainders deterministically by line ID. Mixed inclusive/exclusive adjustment bases reject. Fees require an explicit tax group; tax is computed once per group. Unknown treatment remains an incomplete draft, never zero tax. Zero-rated and exempt are distinct recorded treatments. Inclusive amounts split into net and tax; exclusive tax adds to net. CAPELLA and policy comparisons use net before tax, not payable or deposits. Payment stages allocate the payable amount, with an explicit final balance; they do not create revenue or processing fees.

Examples: 60+40−10+5 =95 net; exclusive10% gives9.50 tax and104.50 payable. Three equal1.00 lines share a1.00 discount as.34/.33/.33 by stable ID. Inclusive110 at10% is100 net+10 tax. Taxable100 plus exempt50 at10% gives150 net+10 tax. Two.05 lines in one10% group round tax once to.01. A named+200 adjustment can change1000 to1200 without inventing quantity. A1200 policy floor remains100 above1100 net even when110 tax makes payable1210.
