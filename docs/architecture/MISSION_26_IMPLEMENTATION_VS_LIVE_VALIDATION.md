Founder sequencing clarification, 2026-09-21: NorthStar's roadmap implementation precedes the founder's planned attorney review of commercial legal documents. Do not make public Retell calling, months of real calls or an attorney-reviewed homepage Web Call a prerequisite to continuing Mission 26 parts. This clarification does not activate a provider, change legal or consent wording, grant source permission, waive security, or turn missing history into a forecast.

## Two different verdicts

**Implementation acceptance** asks whether mounted NorthStar code, guarded readers, calculations, saved runs, correction behavior, paid unavailable states and the isolated demo work according to their contracts. Complete, explicitly fictional source and outcome fixtures can prove deterministic math, source boundaries, future-cutoff discipline, recovery, tenant separation and end-to-end behavior. A controlled synthetic paid tenant is test evidence, not a real paid customer's forecast. Every required exact-head audit, migration, release, deployment, health and founder visual gate remains separately recorded. No absent CI, private-production, physical-device or provider evidence is relabeled as passing.

**Live forecast validation** asks whether a particular tenant has authorized, complete, comparable real history and later finalized outcomes sufficient to evaluate a specific target and method. Until that evidence exists, its numerical forecast stays unavailable. A deployed implementation and a healthy server do not establish real-world forecast accuracy, calibrated probabilities, coverage, caller permission or production readiness for that target. This validation can occur later without reopening sealed Mission 24 or expanding Mission 25.

Mission 26 Parts 4 and 12 may continue toward implementation acceptance using guarded mounted code and isolated synthetic full journeys while the real Retell source is offline. Part 4A still needs its planned source and future-facing saved-output contract or a documented blocker under its existing authority; a pure candidate or static unavailable card alone is not final Part 4 acceptance. If final mission acceptance uses a documented unavailable external-evidence boundary, state precisely which real forecasts remain disabled and which acceptance checks were actually proved. Do not call the live demand forecast accepted or calibrated. The fixed 12-part, 51-slice structure is unchanged.

## Homepage browser Web Call is a different source

The account-free homepage Web Call is intentionally ephemeral: its separate [service](../../src/services/homepageWebCall.js) and [route](../../src/routes/homepageDemo.js) seek verified provider deletion and NorthStar purge before displaying derived demo output, and [configuration](../../src/config.js) requires legal, provider, webhook-isolation and enablement gates. Its route is on a [public page](../../public/index.html) even when the site has no marketing traffic. It can eventually exercise an authorized call experience, but a handful of founder test calls cannot prove historical lead coverage, and purged demo calls must not be imported into Mission 26 as retained business leads or calibration outcomes. A deletion workflow also does not by itself certify every provider retention or secondary-use fact.

Use fictional complete datasets and permitted local/provider-neutral integration tests to build now. Keep the homepage call gates closed until their owning approvals actually exist. If a private founder-only live test is later desired, design and review a separate access and provider boundary; do not silently flip public-page flags or reuse ephemeral demo data as forecast history.
