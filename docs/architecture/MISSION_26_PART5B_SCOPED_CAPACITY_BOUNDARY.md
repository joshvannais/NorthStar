# Mission 26 Part 5B — dimension-scoped capacity prerequisite

Status: bounded, unmounted output-shape candidate. This package produces no capacity number, forecast, source read, scheduling action, worker assignment, asset reservation or customer-facing statement. Final Part 5B acceptance remains open.

Part 3A's `capacity.available_role_hours.v1` needs an exact role identity. Material, asset, travel and fuel targets likewise need item, asset, route or operating-state identities. The released `m26-forecast-output-v1` has no field for these dimensions. Reusing a service key, a generic applicability limit or a source digest as a hidden role/asset identifier would allow unlike evidence to be pooled. `src/forecasting/scopedOutputContract.js` therefore adds a separate `m26-forecast-output-v2` shape with a versioned, digest-checked dimension tuple. Every dimension has an explicit value or `null`; an entirely empty tuple is rejected. Only bounded canonical tokens, UUIDs and digests are accepted. The existing v1 validator still checks horizon, target, unit, amount, source pin, coverage, uncertainty and calibration shape; v1 itself remains unchanged.

The tuple is a **shape**, not proof of identity or applicability. A future target-specific reader must establish which dimensions are required, that a role, crew, location, item, asset and configuration were current at the prediction cutoff, and that the tuple agrees with the authorized source snapshot. The saved run and later actual-outcome join must preserve the tuple, its digest, the definition version and any correction lineage. If the target-specific scope cannot be proved, the output remains unavailable. A calculated digest supplied by an untrusted caller is not source authentication.

Part 5B must consume, not reimplement, Mission 22's approved schedule, crew membership, declared availability, skill, conflict, location and route authority; Mission 20's current workforce and asset records; Mission 23's accepted execution evidence; and Mission 24's approved work requirements where applicable. An active employee does not imply qualification or free time. Declared availability does not prove future attendance. A straight-line distance is not a driving route. An owned machine is not necessarily available, suitable, transported or paired with a qualified operator. Vehicle/equipment conflicts, incomplete route evidence or unreviewed time off must stay explicit. The current Part 5A diagnostic is an unauthenticated present workload position, not a complete backlog reader or a forecast.

Before Part 5B can issue a forecast, an as-of owning-source adapter must prove same-tenant authorization, complete bounded coverage, current revisions, role/crew and asset identities, working-hour calendar and time zone, absences, prior commitments, travel buffers and applicability. It must calculate or withhold capacity at the target's exact grain without double-counting a person in two crews or one shared asset in overlapping jobs. The target-specific output and later actual reader then need Part 3 evaluation and calibration gates. Missing evidence never becomes zero capacity or a confident future date. No advice may create a schedule or assignment; Part 5C owns advisory consequences and Mission 22 retains action authority.

`tests/unit/m26-scoped-output-contract.test.js` verifies v1 compatibility, exact distinct scope digests, frozen output, malformed/hidden input rejection and inherited financial/source safeguards. It does not prove a role source, asset inventory, forecast accuracy, paid/demo UI, migration, production availability or acceptance of Part 5B.
