# Mission 25 Part 10 Slice F — vehicle and equipment calibration

Slice F adds separately consented, tenant-private calibration from five to 100 current reviewed Slice D outcomes for one source and service. The immutable proposal pins current source and outcome permission, every selected observation and its complete underlying adopted-plan, reconciliation, asset-version, ledger and imported-record lineage, the deterministic calculation version and the request identity.

Utilization and operating cost are evaluated independently. A dimension needs at least five current outcomes with one exact unit or currency. Its advisory uses the median actual-to-planned ratio and records the lower and upper quartiles. Incompatible or incomplete dimensions remain unavailable. Maintenance, downtime, condition and availability remain unavailable because current authority has no adopted comparison baseline for them. The service does not infer conversions, exchange rates, engine-on time, productivity, health targets or costs.

Corrections, tombstones, source permission, outcome permission, reconciliation, adopted-plan, target asset or equipment-ledger changes make the saved proposal stale and mask its advice. Revocation hides and blocks derived work. A later grant cannot revive a proposal made under an older consent period.

This API-only slice adds no rendered owner surface. Source lifecycle operations and the Learning Center experience remain Slices G-H. No estimate, price, plan, schedule, job, vehicle, equipment, allocation, reimbursement, payroll record or policy changes automatically.
