## Three different measures

**Seasonality** describes a repeatable change in *verified inbound demand* across comparable calendar periods. It must begin with Part 4A's distinct-lead observations, not raw call counts, customer inquiries or a current dashboard total. The source must certify the entire compared period, tenant, service/area attribution, calendar, time zone, recording lag and correction history. A seasonal adjustment requires a separately versioned method and out-of-sample evaluation under Parts 3B–D. With insufficient complete periods, report unavailable. A fictional demo can illustrate the calculation but cannot calibrate a paid tenant.

**Current backlog** is a present-state fact, not a prediction. A future guarded reader must join the same-tenant approved work/booking authority to Mission 22's current approved schedule and Mission 23's accepted completion/reopening state, all at one pinned cutoff. It must distinguish approved unscheduled work, approved scheduled work, work in progress, completed work and unresolved linkage. Planned hours require an approved scope/plan with a compatible person-hour basis; an appointment duration or crew headcount is not actual worker time. A current backlog count may be known while backlog *hours* remain unavailable. The reader must use immutable source revisions and recheck correction, entitlement and tenant authority. It must not reserve a worker, change a schedule or promote a lead.

Migration 207 and the paid owner/admin `/api/v1/forecast/current-backlog/snapshots` workflow mount a narrow form of that present-state fact. During migration only, deterministic assignment-then-event writer fences drain active writers and prevent a queued writer from entering between projection backfill and trigger installation. Genuine schedule writers then maintain the tenant-private unique current-position projection in the same transaction as accepted-booking and cancellation changes. A serializable capture uses its partial tenant/active index to bound authenticated booking identities to 500 before enrichment without steady-state cross-tenant table locks, then pins the exact immutable schedule and execution revisions behind each private member receipt. It separates approved unscheduled, approved scheduled, in-progress, completed and unresolved linkage counts. A later schedule event or changed execution pin invalidates the receipt instead of silently serving a stale current fact. HTTP returns counts and digests only; appointment, assignment and execution identities remain private.

This is deliberately a known NorthStar subset. It begins with purpose-bound accepted-booking events released after the scheduling bridge, does not backfill incomplete legacy or off-platform work, and cannot prove a complete business backlog. Migration 207 alone returns `plannedPersonMinutes=null` because an appointment duration or crew assignment is not an approved person-hour plan. `knownSubsetOnly=true`, `sourceCoverageComplete=false`, all provider/off-platform/calibration/forecast claims remain false, and the route never schedules, dispatches, promotes or completes work.

Migration 208 and the paid owner/admin `/api/v1/forecast/current-backlog/person-plan-sources/:appointmentId/reviews` workflow add a separate source bridge for an exact current Mission 24 labor plan. An approval binds one authenticated current booking assignment, its opportunity's current approved estimate decision and its current saved labor plan under immutable tenant-private review history. A tenant-and-estimate generation fence advances in the same transaction as each genuine estimate-decision, labor-plan or estimate-revision writer; the approval locks that fence after the shared estimate row so an overlapping writer either orders before the approval and is re-read, or orders after it. The guarded response exposes only the reviewed person-minute total and explicit false coverage/forecast claims; estimate, decision, labor-plan and assignment pins remain private. Any schedule revision, estimate-source revision, estimate-decision revision, labor-plan revision or withdrawal makes the approval stale. An owner/admin must explicitly withdraw or reapprove it, and idempotent writes cannot silently retarget the source.

Migration 209 composes that reviewed source into the existing current-backlog receipt without changing its factual boundary. A serializable capture first takes and retains the tenant subscription row's shared lock, verifies paid owner/admin authority, bounds the current authenticated booking subset to 500, takes every appointment review fence and current assignment lock in deterministic order, then takes the exact Mission 24 estimate and source-generation locks for existing reviews. It rechecks paid authority immediately before persistence, including the current trial clock. Its private manifest records one plan-review state for every active counted booking. `plannedPersonMinutes` is returned only when every approved-unscheduled, approved-scheduled and in-progress member has a current compatible migration 208 review at that one capture. Missing or withdrawn review history yields `reviewed_person_hour_plan_missing`; incompatible or corrected schedule, estimate, decision or labor-plan authority yields `reviewed_person_hour_plan_not_current`; unresolved schedule/execution linkage takes precedence; and a subset with no active work returns `no_active_backlog`. Completed members remain count facts and are excluded from remaining planned minutes.

The saved private plan identities and totals participate in both source and snapshot digests. Read and replay reacquire the same appointment, assignment, estimate and generation-fence order before checking currentness. Any later genuine schedule, estimate-decision, estimate-revision or labor-plan mutation makes the whole saved receipt `source_stale` and withholds counts, digests and person minutes. Runtime retains only the guarded capture/read entries; the private plan manifest and internal source-lock helper remain inaccessible. Human plan reviews use the separate bounded review allowance rather than consuming the four-per-hour source-capture allowance.

This composition is still neither complete backlog coverage nor a forecast. It includes only the bounded post-bridge NorthStar booking subset, requires explicit owner/admin reviews, does not backfill legacy or off-platform work and never infers person-hours from appointment duration or headcount. `knownSubsetOnly=true`, `sourceCoverageComplete=false`, `offPlatformCoverageVerified=false`, `providerCoverageVerified=false`, `probabilityCalibrated=false`, `forecastIssued=false` and `paidNumericServing=false` remain mandatory even when a private planned-minute total is available.

**Pipeline-to-scheduled work** is a future transition. A customer expressing interest, requesting an estimate, preferring a date or receiving a preliminary estimate remains pipeline; none is a confirmed booking. Only the first accepted booking transition can be evaluated as an outcome. A forward probability requires Part 4B's exact frozen cohort, finalized historical outcomes, source coverage, target-specific sufficiency/calibration and saved-run governance. Until then, show pipeline and confirmed scheduled work as separate observed categories. Do not add an uncalibrated fraction of open leads to booked work or label it guaranteed work.

## Minimum additive interface for future implementation

Reuse Part 2A's purpose-bound immutable snapshot and current-read invalidation pattern. Add only source-specific guarded manifests for the owning Mission 22/23 and approved-work identities; do not widen the existing approved-estimate snapshot purpose or rewrite those source records. Each manifest should pin organization, identity/revision/digest, occurrence and record times, cutoff, approval/completion state, relevant schedule window, and explicit linkage status. Missing or contradictory links remain unavailable. The current known backlog and any future transition forecast need separate output identities so a fact cannot be mistaken for a prediction. Part 11 stores a future forecast run; Mission 28 owns any later automatic action.

No generic seasonal coefficient, fixed booking probability or retrospective "forecast" may be invented to fill the current history gap. Existing investor forecast and legacy in-memory opportunity multipliers are not Mission 26 evidence. A paid tenant with no verified history should see a plain-language unavailable reason, while the isolated demo uses clearly fictional source receipts and no cross-tenant learning.

## Evidence required before a numerical claim

Test duplicate opportunity/booking events; a lead calling twice; a preferred slot versus an approved booking; an approved booking later canceled or rescheduled; a job completed, reopened or changed in scope; an approved job with missing hours; DST and changed business hours; incomplete lead-channel coverage; late source correction; consent revocation; different tenant identities; and a future booking recorded after the forecast cutoff. Compare a saved future forecast against later finalized outcomes without rewriting either. Synthetic tests can establish calculation and refusal behavior, but not real seasonality, calibrated conversion, live provider coverage or production accuracy.
