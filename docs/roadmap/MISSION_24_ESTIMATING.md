## Reconciled meaning of Capella

The explicit accepted name is Capella Risk Lens. Later assistant summaries compressed Capella into document composition, but no founder cancellation of Risk Lens was recovered. Preserve both the private risk capability and the separately required branded quote capability. Broader naming of quote composition is not an exclusive replacement for Risk Lens.

Authorized internal Risk Lens users must be able to see relevant private costs, margins and downside analysis. The privacy exclusion applies to the contractor's customer quote/export, not to that internal analysis. “Customer-facing” in an earlier discussion can mean NorthStar's business-user customer; it does not grant access to the contractor's customer.
