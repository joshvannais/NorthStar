# Mission 27 Part 6C — invoice customer view and protected interaction boundary

## Frozen scope

Part 6C owns customer view and download, invoice questions, billing-detail
correction requests, and delivery and view acknowledgements. These acts do not
grant invoice mutation authority. A customer mutation requires the exact
scoped session, recipient-verification policy, current server-side target, and
origin and CSRF protection frozen by Part 1. Part 6D separately owns delivery,
resend, revoke, recovery, Mission 28 handoff and complete Part 6 rendered
acceptance.

Released Part 6B is intentionally value-free. No immutable issued document
exists, so it created no high-entropy token, hash, public lookup, expiration,
exact-version binding, revocation record or minimized customer session. A
private staff session, demo workspace, caller-supplied token, draft preview or
Mission 24 proposal link cannot substitute for that authority. Part 6C must
therefore mount no public customer route and accept no customer mutation.

## Exact-current readiness projection

The internal paid owner/administrator read re-enters the released Part 6B
repository. That chain reconstructs the current private Part 5 source and
strictly validates the value-free Part 6A document and Part 6B public-session
contracts. Part 6C discards the upstream value and returns
`m27-invoice-customer-portal-v1` with state `unavailable`, reason
`issued_invoice_unavailable` and a null value.

The contract keeps view, download, question, billing-detail correction request,
delivery acknowledgement and view acknowledgement as six distinct actions.
Every action is unavailable, value-free and unauthorized. It also names the
missing exact public session, recipient verification and mutation origin/CSRF
boundary. No public view or download, mutation route, acknowledgement, invoice
mutation, customer/work/amount/document/digest/receipt/token value, provider
call or receiver effect is created.

The account-free demo rebuilds and validates the fictional Part 6B boundary
from one demo-workspace revision, then returns the same minimized contract with
`mode=demo` and `fictional=true`. It cannot query paid data, mint a fictional
customer credential, or fall back to a provider.

## Acceptance evidence and deferred work

Part 6C acceptance requires strict contract, repository and route evidence plus
real mounted paid and demo reads against fresh PostgreSQL 17 through migration
272. The campaign covers deterministic replay, owner/admin and tenant
isolation, exact source currentness, private `no-store` caching, no cookie,
absence of every public/mutation method, stale-source denial and zero database
residue. No migration 273 is required because no session, question, correction
request or acknowledgement may exist.

There is no customer-rendered surface to exercise while the issued-document
and scoped-session prerequisites are unavailable, so Part 6C creates no browser
or visual claim. Part 6D remains unstarted. Private production, recipient legal
review, external providers and receivers, live money, hosted CI, physical
Safari/devices, disaster restore and founder visual approval remain unavailable
unless independently evidenced.
