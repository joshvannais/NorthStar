## Explicit billable-work review

`GET` and `POST /api/v1/customer-financial/editable-estimates/:estimateId/billable-work-review` let a current paid-tenant owner or administrator select one source class, inspect its exact source evidence, and record why that source is invoiceable. A final balance requires the exact current Part 3A Mission 24 commercial basis and current Part 3B Mission 23 completion and handoff evidence. A pre-work deposit requires one exact accepted Mission 24 deposit schedule and satisfies the frozen amount and percentage bounds without claiming completion. Milestones remain value-free as `milestone_authority_unsupported` until Mission 23 supplies separately accepted milestone or work-package authority.

The database resolves current membership, subscription, recent authentication, CSRF, the action-specific `customer_financial.billable_work.review` capability, frozen `mission27.launch.v1` policy, source class, source identity, source and authority digests, amount, and control mode. An exact amount through USD 10,000 uses single control; a larger supported amount through USD 100,000 requires two distinct authorized reviewers. The source decision remains nonfinancial: it grants no invoice, provider, receiver, customer-contact, scheduling, work, or learning action.

The accepted review is append-only and tenant-private. Exact request replay is bound to the actor, session, request key, source decision and grant revision. A changed request conflicts. Commercial and per-work database fences require a current source re-read before the review commits; reopened work, changed commercial evidence, entitlement loss, superseded authorization, or a different source digest clears older value. Exact zero remains a supported amount and is distinct from unavailable evidence.

## Shared mobile Polaris quote review

The mounted customer detail loads `m27-polaris-quote-journey-v1` for paid, authenticated owner or administrator sessions. It projects the current Part 3A Mission 24 basis into one editable view over the same estimate and source identities. Every option name, scope, validity period, assumption, line description, quantity, unit, and exact USD price is editable. Users may add or remove ordered lines and up to three options within the frozen limits. The browser performs no floating-point money arithmetic; exact minor units and deterministic server digests bind every save.

Draft saves require the exact current edit capability and optimistic draft revision. A change invalidates the prior proposal, signature and handoff states. The current draft table deliberately does not claim Part 3D's immutable original-versus-human revision history; Part 3D remains responsible for that history and complete invalidation lifecycle.

Multi-option proposal review reuses Mission 24 proposal authority and binds one proposal ID, revision and digest to one exact current draft revision and digest. A proposal at or below USD 10,000 needs one authorized review; a larger supported proposal needs two different authorized reviewers. A competing proposal for the same draft fails closed. The first review remains discoverable after reload so the independent reviewer examines the exact same proposal. Automatic delivery is false.

External typed-name signature remains named and value-free as `legal_review_unavailable`; professional legal review is still required before external signature launch. Accepted-estimate-to-work remains named and value-free as `work_handoff_receiver_unavailable` because `mission23.work_intake.review.v1` has no accepted receiving adapter. The journey creates no appointment, assignment, reservation, dispatch, execution, invoice, payment, provider request, receiver mutation, or learning write. Mission 24 retains estimate, price, proposal, option, signature and acceptance authority; Mission 22 scheduling and dispatch; Mission 23 work intake, execution and evidence; Mission 25 tenant-private learning; Mission 32 the later deep calculator over the shared canonical model.

The editor is keyboard operable, announces loading/success/failure, uses explicit labels and stable focus targets, and inherits responsive light/dark styles. It is loaded on the shared paid shells but refuses the account-free demo surface. The isolated fictional demo counterpart remains a later Mission 27 acceptance slice.
