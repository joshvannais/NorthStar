The first provider-independent paid journey should use the cataloged, unscoped **`revenue.approved_price_flow.v1`** target, which measures future human-approved commercial price decisions, not earned revenue or cash. Its first missing prerequisite is a bounded, guarded Mission 24 decision **event-flow/as-of reader** carrying price, currency, actual decision time and correction/withdrawal lineage under current tenant and purpose authority. The existing Part 6A arithmetic can consume a proven historical event series, but it is not a forecast method. The migration 136 snapshot contains latest decision pins rather than a complete event-flow price series, so it cannot silently serve as that reader. After source coverage and event semantics are proved in a controlled synthetic paid tenant, register a deterministic future-facing method and implement forecast-specific paid permission, atomic saved-run issue/read/currentness, explanation and review-only receiving handoff. Retell lead counts retain the separate Part 4A consent, retention, reviewed lead identity and provider-coverage gates. No missing period is zero by default, and no sealed Mission 24 contract is changed.

Acceptance tests must traverse the **mounted** path with current owner/admin and denial for member, wrong tenant, expired subscription, wrong purpose, stale/corrected/revoked source and replayed or concurrent issue. They must pin cutoff before future facts; preserve exact source, settings, target, algorithm, output and run digests; compare a controlled rerun; prove the customer-safe/private-data boundary; and show a reviewed handoff that makes no receiving change until that mission independently authorizes it. The same route with incomplete real history remains unavailable. Part 12B separately proves isolated fictional demo behavior; Parts 12C–F own recovery, visual, independent audit and release/founder gates. Provider, CI, private-production and physical-device evidence must be reported separately if unavailable.
