Status: guarded owner-reviewed commercial-booking withdrawal cohort candidate in implementation, not the canonical booking-cancellation target and not an accepted probability forecast. NorthStar has not operated a public Retell source long enough to establish complete, comparable lead history. Attorney review remains a separate prerequisite to public calling. This expected lack of live history does not require recovery of old setup calls and does not prevent synthetic validation or independent work in later Mission 26 slices. Paid predictions remain unavailable until their own source and evaluation gates pass.

Part 3A defines four different as-of cohort transitions: qualification, accepted estimate request, accepted booking, and booking cancellation. A candidate cohort is frozen at its prediction cutoff. Only its first qualifying transition strictly after that cutoff and inside the stated horizon can enter its numerator. A new lead or appointment created after the cutoff cannot enter the denominator. A reschedule is not automatically a cancellation; a preliminary estimate is not an accepted estimate request; a desired appointment is not an accepted booking. Each target requires a separate versioned owner-source reader and later finalized outcome reader. No current caller-supplied summary proves those facts.

`src/forecasting/transitionRateDiagnostic.js` performs one narrow calculation over supplied fictional or future guarded finalized cohort summaries. It validates tenant-wide reporting windows, cutoffs, mutually distinct period starts, nonzero eligible denominators, complete outcomes, units of count, matching prediction lead time, and same-context comparability. It pools transition counts over eligible counts, so a tiny cohort does not receive the same weight as a large one. Missing, revoked, incomparable, zero-denominator or absent cohorts return a named unavailable state. The result is explicitly `descriptive_only`, `sourceAuthenticated: false`, `confidence: unavailable`, and `forecastIssued: false`. It has no route, saved run or UI and grants no access to a tenant record. Tests use fictional cohorts; they prove arithmetic and refusal behavior, not a business's transition probability.

Migration 202 and the paid internal `/api/v1/forecast/transition-cohorts/commercial-booking-withdrawals` workflow mount the narrow `commercial.owner_reviewed_booking_withdrawal` target without widening that diagnostic into a forecast. An owner or administrator chooses an exact historical cutoff and ended horizon. The database freezes only appointments with an immutable `first_booking_reviewed` record at or before the cutoff and excludes anything first reviewed later. A commercial review already withdrawn at the cutoff is not eligible. Only the first owner-reviewed withdrawal strictly after the cutoff and no later than the horizon enters the numerator. A correction inside the outcome horizon makes the capture unavailable instead of silently changing the cohort. The saved receipt keeps the private appointment membership in a revoked table, while the HTTP projection returns only counts and digests.

Capture requires a live paid owner/admin session, CSRF, replay-safe bounded request serialization, idempotency and the existing commercial-booking source lock. The ended horizon prevents future leakage. Reads recheck for any source row inserted inside that horizon after the saved high-water mark and withhold the result as stale. The source is authenticated only as NorthStar's owner-reviewed commercial-booking review history. Its `booking_cancelled` action does not change Mission 22 appointment state and does not prove operational cancellation, so this candidate cannot authenticate `demand.booking_cancellation.v1`, cannot establish whether a booking remained at risk at cutoff, and cannot satisfy Part 3A. `sourceCoverageComplete`, `offPlatformCoverageVerified`, `providerCoverageVerified`, `probabilityCalibrated`, `forecastIssued` and `paidNumericServing` remain false. Empty history is `unavailable`; it never becomes a zero probability.
