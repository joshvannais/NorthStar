# Mission 26 Part 4C — seasonality, backlog and demand-to-schedule boundary

Status: target-complete scoped implementation candidate through additive migration 222; not canonically accepted or released. Migrations 207–210 remain the accepted current-backlog foundation. Migration 222 adds separate, tenant-private research authorities for one Retell-only seasonal candidate and one same-population pipeline first-booking count. It does not change migrations or routes through 221 and does not start Part 4D.

## Three outputs that must remain separate

Part 4C has three independently identified outputs. A current backlog fact is a present-state observation. A seasonal origin is a saved future research candidate for `demand.inbound_leads.v1`. A pipeline origin is a saved future research candidate for `demand.pipeline_first_accepted_bookings.v1`. None may be added to another or presented as booked work, guaranteed work, a production forecast, or an automatic commercial action.

All public paid projections are restricted to owners and admins and withhold private numeric outputs, output digests and evaluation metrics. They report `researchOnly=true`, `realForecastEligible=false`, `forecastIssued=false`, `paidNumericServing=false` and `forecastServingEnabled=false`. Member, unpaid, expired, missing and cross-tenant access fails closed without disclosing private identities.

## Current backlog fact

Migration 222 reuses the guarded migration 207–210 current-backlog authority instead of interpreting backlog as a forecast. ## Seasonal Retell research candidate

The seasonal authority is prospective. An owner or admin must first create a purpose-fixed coverage epoch after the exact Business Profile anchor and activation, active Retell integration and explicit Retell source-consent grant exist. The epoch pins those immutable identities, versions, digests, activation times, tenant-local calendar and time zone under the real profile, consent, integration and Retell source fences. Training cannot use a month beginning before all of those activations. No uncertain history is backfilled.

A separate append-only owner/admin review must approve the registered `m26-seasonal-two-cycle-open-minute-v1` method. Rejection blocks capture. A later method review, profile or time-zone change, integration change, consent revocation, source correction or certification revision makes the saved projection unavailable for current use and requires explicit recapture or reevaluation. Registration alone never approves a method.

For the next tenant-local calendar month, the server selects exactly 24 contiguous completed local months, from horizon minus 25 months through horizon minus 2 months. Callers cannot supply periods, members, counts, cutoffs, horizons, manifests, algorithms, uncertainty or actuals. Each month must have the latest current migration 220 certification over the same epoch, Business Profile, calendar, time zone, Retell integration and consent lineage. Every certification must be recorded at least seven elapsed days after that local month ended. The seven-day rule is a conservative versioned recording-close rule; it is not a statistical, provider or legal assurance. Missing, reordered, duplicate, late, revoked, stale or differently sourced evidence refuses the entire capture.

The receipt references at most 1,000 calls and 256 KiB per certified source month through migration 220, while the Part 4C origin pins only 24 ordered certification identities, revisions, digests, counts and elapsed/open-minute exposures. It never embeds the possible 24,000 raw-call references. Calendar and open minutes come from the exact pinned Business Profile and preserve IANA time-zone and DST behavior; every denominator must be positive.

The deterministic method forms two 12-month cycles. Each cycle's total leads divided by total open minutes is its annual baseline. The same calendar month's rate in each cycle is compared with its own baseline. Both above yields `repeated_high`; both below yields `repeated_low`; both exactly equal yields `repeated_neutral`. Opposing or one-neutral comparisons yield `inconclusive`, which is unavailable and persists no origin or point. Two authenticated zero cycles yield `authenticated_complete_zero_no_signal`, which is complete zero rather than proof of a seasonal pattern. An eligible private point is the mean of the two same-calendar-month rates multiplied by the future horizon month's pinned open minutes with the registered rounding rule.

The immutable origin is saved before the horizon. Migration 222 separately observes each genuine completed Retell operation, including canonical occurrence and later recording/visibility time, under the Retell source tables' writer boundary. Every training period and evaluated horizon pins that complete observation generation. A late-recorded call whose canonical occurrence belongs to a pinned month therefore stales that exact use even before a replacement certification is issued; calls outside the pinned periods do not. Only after the horizon plus the same seven-day close lag can an append-only evaluation pin the exact current certified horizon-month outcome and private error metric. A later outcome certification or source-observation revision makes the prior evaluation stale and requires a new evaluation revision; the saved origin is never rewritten. Mounted lifecycle evidence uses only the private database-owner `canonical_forecast_demand_schedule_test_clock_v1_set` interface with the explicit `northstar.m26_part4c_disposable_clock=enabled` session guard. The clock is monotonic, is denied to `PUBLIC` and the runtime role, and production calls fall back to the real clock. That guarded synthetic time advancement can establish ordering behavior, but it does not establish natural seasonality, statistical significance, empirical accuracy, confidence or calibration.

## Pipeline first-accepted-booking research count

The pipeline target is a count over one exact current NorthStar opportunity risk set, not Retell volume multiplied by marginal transition rates. It does not use the Part 4B qualification, estimate-request, booking or cancellation probabilities, because those authorities do not establish compatible conditional funnel populations. Retell coupling remains unavailable without an exact population bridge.

A prospective purpose-fixed epoch pins the Business Profile authority and the migration 206 opportunity-eligibility and migration 203 accepted-booking sources. Migration 222 attaches prospective visibility recording directly to every genuine migration 206 operation-completion writer and every genuine migration 203 accepted-booking writer under their shared tenant fences; it does not depend on an incidental reader to activate old rows. Target membership and first-event ordering use the canonical source occurrence (`completed_at` for eligibility and the approved schedule mutation time for bookings), while the separately pinned observation time proves prospective visibility and detects late arrival. First accepted bookings must occur strictly after the lower boundary and before the exclusive upper boundary; observation delay never changes that target definition, and a later cancellation does not erase the first accepted-booking fact. The disposable owner-only clock boundary reaches those exact source timestamp acquisition points through narrowly scoped migration 222 triggers only when its private guard and clock are present. A second owner-only source-clock singleton can hold the owning source occurrence fixed while the observation clock advances, which makes delayed visibility independently testable; both tables and setters are denied to runtime and `PUBLIC`, and production leaves them empty so migration 203/206 keep their supplied wall-clock timestamps. A separate append-only owner/admin review must approve `m26-pipeline-first-booking-pooled-v1`, including the eligibility definition, fixed 30 elapsed-day horizon, exclusive upper boundary, formula and rounding. Capture drains those genuine eligibility and schedule-event writers, rechecks access and paid status immediately before persistence, and refuses without a partial row if any source changes.

The server selects two complete prior 30-day training windows and one current eligible, unbooked cohort at the prediction cutoff. All three use the same source, eligibility rule and strict event timing: a first accepted booking must occur after the relevant start and before the exclusive end. Post-cutoff entrants are excluded from the future risk set. A cancellation or reschedule does not erase the historical fact that a first booking was accepted. Callers cannot choose members, truncate windows, supply counts or actuals, or move the horizon.

The private research count is current eligible members multiplied by the pooled historical first-booking fraction. An authenticated empty current cohort yields complete zero. A positive current cohort with a zero historical denominator is unavailable. Each complete population removes every prior-booked member before applying its 501-row overflow sentinel, so UUID ordering cannot hide later unbooked members behind already-booked candidates. Across both training arrays and the current risk array together, the origin permits at most 500 ordered member identities and 256 KiB of encoded evidence; excess refuses atomically without truncation or a row. It pins both training cohorts, exact source orders and digests, the profile/context authority and approved method identity. Late pre-cutoff source changes stale the input. Authorized post-cutoff entrants and outcomes do not rewrite or invalidate the frozen risk set.

After the exclusive 30-day horizon ends, an append-only evaluation examines only the saved members and pins each exact first accepted-booking event, the complete outcome high-water and private error metric. It distinguishes authenticated zero from missing or incomplete outcome coverage. A relevant lineage gap or source correction makes current evaluation unavailable; a changed complete outcome digest requires an explicit new evaluation key and revision. The origin and earlier evaluation remain immutable.

## Concurrency, idempotency and privilege boundary

Migration 222 uses deterministic purpose, profile, consent, source and origin/evaluation lock order. Genuine in-flight Retell consent/certification/profile writers and opportunity/schedule writers either commit before the final server-selected read or order after it and invalidate current use. Seasonal evaluation reads take the same horizon-month certification fence as certification mutation, reread the latest revision after waiting and recheck access before returning. Epoch and method-review replays likewise recheck paid actor authority after their fence waits; an epoch retry also revalidates the exact current profile, consent and integration identities before returning the old immutable row. Bounds are checked before enrichment where possible, and every refusal rolls back without a partial origin or evaluation.

Every write is CSRF protected and idempotent. An exact retry returns the immutable row only while its source, context, method and access remain current; a retry after relevant mutation returns stale with `refreshRequired=true` and does not append a row. Child operations hash the entire valid parent key plus a stable discriminator before producing a bounded key, so two long parent keys with the same prefix remain distinct. All read and replay paths recheck current actor, membership, session and subscription authority after any source-lock wait and before returning existence or timing metadata. Storage tables and internal clock, calculation, cohort, currentness and outcome helpers deny `PUBLIC` and runtime access; runtime receives only guarded entry functions. Startup verifies all eight primary receipt immutability guards, all four visibility immutability guards, the exact prospective eligibility, booking, certification and Retell-call capture triggers, their owning tables/functions/enabled modes, the guarded source-clock boundary and private privileges. It fails closed if any migration, trigger, entry function or helper boundary is missing, disabled or misdirected.
