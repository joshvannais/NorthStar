# Mission 25 Part 10 Slice H — vehicle and equipment Learning Center

The paid owner and administrator Learning Center now presents native vehicle and equipment checkout-time consent and a distinct external vehicle and equipment source category alongside labor and travel. The bounded PostgreSQL inventory keeps identical source labels separate by category and lists only the current tenant's sources and service groups.

For a selected vehicle and equipment source, the page opens the existing guarded consent, evidence, explicit reference-review, source-operation, asset-health and calibration authorities. It never reads a protected table. Owners can review job, vehicle and equipment links, separately permit utilization and operating-cost comparisons, separately permit maintenance and downtime summaries, and review dimension-specific advisory calibration. Checkout duration is not described as engine-on time. Missing or incompatible dimensions remain unavailable, and the page states that maintenance and downtime do not establish condition or availability.

Deletion recovery is explicit. While a deletion request remains active, the page blocks the source-grant control and directs the owner to cancel deletion first. Cancellation and a new source-consent period do not revive deleted source detail or earlier learning authority. Current matches, observations and proposals must be reviewed again through their owning workflows.

The isolated demo contains fictional, read-only labor, travel and vehicle/equipment records. It performs no paid mutation and cannot enter paid learning authority. Paid actions remain owner or administrator only, tenant private, current-session and CSRF protected, revision pinned and idempotent.

The page uses plain business wording, visible loading/error/ready states, a skip link, live status region, labeled controls, keyboard focus styles, responsive source and dimension cards, bounded tables and dark/light theme tokens. Reload starts at the top. Request-generation guards prevent older source, health and calibration responses from replacing a newer selection.

Nothing in this experience changes an estimate, price, plan, route, schedule, job, vehicle, equipment, maintenance plan, allocation, reimbursement, payroll record or business policy. Provider credentials, provider-specific connections, physical-device evidence, manual assistive-technology evidence and the founder's visual verdict remain outside source-controlled acceptance.
