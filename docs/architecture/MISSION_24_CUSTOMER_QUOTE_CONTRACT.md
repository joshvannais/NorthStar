## Required composition lifecycle

A future composition begins from an exact current approved human decision and selected estimate. An authorized person reviews customer-visible scope, commercial items/adjustments, issuer identity, terms and applicable tax treatment. A preview is a draft, not permission to send or a commercial approval by itself. Finalization must record the explicit human decision about that exact composition, and document generation must bind its immutable content and renderer version. A historical document remains distinguishable from the current approved revision.

**Identity and approval.** Quote composition references exact tenant/customer/estimate/source pins and immutable current human decision id/revision/digest. A commercial quote revision is a separate approved document snapshot, not mutation of canonical_estimates or a reinterpretation of the057 decision. Before future finalization/download generation, current owner/admin access and expected source, human decision and composition versions must be checked. Withdrawn/changed decisions prevent generating a new current quote. Historical documents must retain their historical basis and status. Previously downloaded bytes cannot be recalled; do not promise that capability. Mere local preview is not sending, customer acceptance or charging.

**Commercial reconciliation.** Use explicit approved customer charges; machine lines may be offered as a review starting point, never silently approved. Preserve missing versus zero and exact currency. If line subtotal1000.00 differs from human total1200.00, require an explicitly named +200.00 adjustment or reviewed replacement lines; never distribute200 across labor/materials without authorization. Negative adjustment/discount similarly requires explicit review and valid totals. Quantities/units/rates appear only when supported and approved; their absence is not permission to fabricate them. Grouping cannot change sums. Explicit fees/discounts/deposits/tax have distinct semantics: a deposit is payment schedule, not a duplicate charge. Null tax remains unknown; old machine tax is not reused on a changed approved base. Future Part6/8 must choose supported jurisdiction/tax authority and fail clearly on unsupported cases, without assuming a provider or legal conclusion now.

**Private/public separation.** Customer allowlist: approved work descriptions, approved commercial quantities/units/rates/charges, groups/subtotals/adjustments/tax and total where supported, reviewed customer/job display details, issuer display identity/contact/logo, approved terms and dates. Exclude internal costs/margin/profit/Risk Lens reasoning/evidence diagnostics, IDs/digests/session/actor metadata by default. The latest founder permits deliberate authorized disclosure; that needs a separate explicit preview/decision, not automatic inclusion from internal review. Do not invent a new estimator role: currently authorized paid roles are owner/admin.

**Issuer and branding.** Existing profile raw company fields include name/dba/email/phone/website/logo/taxId/timeZone/currency ([businessProfileAdapter](../../src/services/businessProfileAdapter.js)47–55); the user edits company identity/logo in [Business Profile](../../public/dashboard/business-profile.html)207–215. Logo is a URL field, not verified embedded asset storage. Future quote snapshot must pin an explicitly reviewed current issuer/profile identity separately from the historical calculation profile; changing branding must not rewrite cost provenance. Only chosen public contact/display fields are copied; taxId is not a default public header. Real paid business or fictional demo business is prominent at top, using established theme/font. Authentic platform assets include [authentic logo asset](../../public/assets/northstar-logo.svg); verify the current branded host usage before selecting the exact render asset. Tiny but readable NorthStar mark+name at very bottom right, sufficient contrast at normal print scale. No invented substitute logo or dominant second header. Missing/failed company logo gets a clear text-brand fallback and review, never an external image fetch assumption. Remote logo embedding/download policy requires bounded validation, no provider/storage feature implied.

**Shared demo.** One commercial projection and document renderer, shared validation/rounding/layout. Paid auth/storage and demo token/expiry/reset/caps remain separate adapters; synthetic identity/pins cannot grant paid access. Demo create→preview→download must work through ordinary controls as soon as paid equivalent ships. Fictional issuer/customer/data, clearly simulated document provenance, same supported itemization/tax limits. No simulation controls in paid, no private endpoint calls from demo, no deferral toMission31.

**Future persistence/recovery.** A finalized reproducible document needs immutable composition content/version and approved issuer/itemization/terms/disclosure pins, plus document-renderer version; the existing057 scope/price ledger alone does not provide it. Specify these responsibilities now, but defer exact new table/migration/endpoint shape until Part6/8 runtime scope is reconciled against current main. Any new durable document/commercial revision ledger needs separate migration, concurrency/idempotency, startup/lock impacts, data-preserving paused/forward-fix plan and populated disposable recovery evidence. Never edit applied057 or inherit its one-release risk disposition. Storage, signed customer URLs, email delivery, payment and tax providers are not required or authorized by this planning package.
