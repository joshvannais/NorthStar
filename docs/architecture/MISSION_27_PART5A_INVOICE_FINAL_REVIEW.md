# Mission 27 Part 5A — invoice final-review authority boundary

Part 5A mounts the first paid owner/administrator final-review boundary without weakening the released Mission 27 launch contract. The guarded read accepts one exact current Part 4 handoff receipt and estimate. It reuses the released Part 4E PostgreSQL entry point to revalidate the current tenant, actor, subscription and exact source. It then discards that private projection before constructing the response.

The final review cannot yet be authorized. The frozen `customer_financial.invoice.approve` capability requires `recent_auth_mfa`, but the released account/session authority has no server-owned recent-authentication fact or accepted MFA ceremony. Session creation, `last_used_at`, email verification, role and browser claims do not satisfy that assurance. The frozen launch policy also does not define supported invoice due-term or delivery-choice values, and the released Part 4 preview retains named unavailable amount-due and professional-tax evidence. Part 5A does not invent any of them.

Every authorized read therefore returns `m27-invoice-final-review-v1` with state `unavailable`, reason `recent_auth_mfa_unavailable`, a null value, the required capability and assurance, bounded names of the missing evidence, and every approval, invoice, issue and receivable authorization false. It exposes no customer, work, amount, currency, line, source identity, receipt identity, digest, due term or delivery choice. There is no final-review mutation route, capability issuer, grant, request, approval or receipt table. No explicit-confirmation action is presented while the prerequisites are absent.

This is a fail-closed local implementation of the original Part 5A boundary, not an invoice approval. A later separately reviewed slice may add a genuine assurance ceremony and separately versioned due-term/delivery policy; it must then revalidate the complete current Part 4 authority inside the effect transaction and implement the frozen single/dual-control rules. Part 5B cannot assign a number or issue from this unavailable state.

Migrations remain through 272 with no migration 273. The boundary creates no customer document, invoice, receivable, delivery, communication, provider, receiver, accounting, payment, schedule, work, learning or live-money effect. Live-money validation remains deferred until the first real paying customer. Professional tax review, private production, hosted CI, production load, physical Safari/devices, manual acceptance, disaster restore and founder visual approval remain unavailable.
