# Mission 25 Part 13C complete per-job outcome summary

Part 13C prepares one immutable summary from one exact current Part 13A graph and its current Part 13B evaluation. The summary always addresses labor, travel, equipment, materials, scope and available external financial evidence. Each area is marked independently as recorded, unavailable or needing review.

Recorded source values stay in the shape supplied by their accepted outcome authority. The summary does not combine overlapping sources, select a preferred source, convert units or currencies, infer a missing value, or treat unavailable evidence as zero. When more than one source covers the same claim slot, every current source remains separate and the area needs review. The customer outcome domain remains available to the graph and evaluation authorities but is outside the six-area summary frozen for Slice C.

The summary pins the exact graph and evaluation identities, revisions, canonical and source digests, graph-permission period, deterministic calculation version and request identity. It re-resolves every graph node through its accepted guarded outcome read before saving or displaying detail. A newer graph or evaluation, source correction or tombstone, reviewed-link change, target change, purpose-permission change or graph-permission change makes the prior summary stale and masks its detail. Revocation blocks reads and delayed retries. A later grant begins an empty period and cannot revive an earlier summary.

Only current owners and administrators can use the guarded read and build entries. Runtime cannot read summary storage or execute its resolver, composition, validation, projection or trigger helpers. The summary is advisory and cannot change a customer, lead, appointment, job, estimate, price, schedule, dispatch decision, labor record, vehicle, equipment record, material record, project, financial record or company setting. Cross-job proposals, previews, adoption and lifecycle propagation remain Slices D-G. Native NorthStar invoice, payment, collection and accounting evidence remains unavailable until Mission 27.

This slice adds no rendered page. API messages use plain business language and capitalization. The paid and isolated-demo experience, responsive and accessibility review, physical-device evidence, manual assistive-technology review and founder visual verdict remain Slice H or separately unavailable evidence.
