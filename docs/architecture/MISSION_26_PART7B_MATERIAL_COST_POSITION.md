# Mission 26 Part 7B — planned material-line cost prerequisite

The unmounted `src/forecasting/materialCostPositionDiagnostic.js` reuses the sealed Mission 24 material-plan calculator for quantity, unit, waste, unit price and per-line cents rounding. It accepts only the source-aware V3/V4 plan shapes, checks that each persisted plan currency matches the reporting currency, and inspects the existing M24 price and (for V4) reported availability evidence against the proposed work dates. Claimed incomplete/revoked coverage, truncation, a cross-period or unknown work window, a missing/stale/conflicting price source, or an explicitly short or stale V4 supply position withholds the number. A complete empty **claimed** set may yield zero only as a deterministic hypothetical.

This is the sum of material **line costs** in the pinned plans. It does not include delivery, purchasing fees, taxes, internal transfers, disposal or other job costs. A supplier statement does not reserve stock, and a source label does not authenticate a quote. V3 has no availability record; V4's reported sufficient quantity is still not verified current inventory or a future guarantee. The output therefore always says `deliveryFeesIncluded: false`, `taxTreatmentVerified: false`, `inventoryVerified: false`, `supplierAvailabilityVerified: false`, `sourceAuthenticated: false`, `learnedWasteApplied: false`, and `forecastIssued: false`. It changes no M24 calculation, source review, availability or adoption authority.

Before Part 7B can issue an authorized forecast, an owning-source adapter must prove plan adoption/currentness, corrections and complete as-of visibility, job/calendar attribution, tenant/role access, relevant supplier price and availability freshness, purchasing minima/fees/tax/transport inclusion, actual stock and reservation semantics where available, and compatibility with any M25 learned material outcomes without applying waste twice. Missing source data remains unavailable. Imported source evidence must not become a live quote or committed inventory. Part 7B and Part 7 acceptance remain open; no route, migration or customer-facing surface is introduced here.

Focused tests cover sealed M24 arithmetic reuse, V3 and V4, shortage and stale supply, missing/conflicting price, temporal and tenant separation, duplicate identities, currency relabeling, bounded lists and nested accessor refusal. Later acceptance requires role-separated PostgreSQL, authenticated paid/demo source and correction journeys, calibrated evaluation as applicable, and independent release review.

## Mounted completion

Migration 241 and `GET /api/v1/forecast/material-cost/current` now mount the smallest source-backed Part 7B forecast for the next 30 days. The read is owner/admin only, tenant-private, session-fenced, `READ COMMITTED`, bounded to 500 active backlog positions, and guarded by the released scheduling, commercial-booking, M24 estimate/adoption and M25 learning locks. Each included job must be currently scheduled inside the period, retain a current owner-confirmed booked-work receipt, point to the latest approved estimate revision, and retain one exact current adopted V4 material plan. A missing, replaced, stale or mismatched source makes the forecast unavailable rather than carrying an old number forward.

The calculation is still the sealed M24 per-line arithmetic: recorded quantity times recorded unit price plus the recorded waste percentage, rounded in exact cents per line. Every price record and owner-recorded availability statement must apply to the service and material specification, use the reporting currency and unit, apply to the reviewed job, and cover the scheduled job dates. The mounted result reports current planned base cost, planned waste cost and total planned material-line cost across the qualified booked jobs.

The response and the compact row inside the existing **Cost & operational risk** drill-in keep the source limits visible. This is a planned material-line cost forecast only. NorthStar has not verified purchase orders, fees, taxes, transport or delivery, actual on-hand inventory, stock reservation, supplier identity, or inventory value. Applicable adopted M25 material learning would require a separate compatible adjustment authority; no learned multiplier is silently applied. Probability, calibrated ranges and automatic purchasing actions remain unavailable. The isolated demo uses fictional local data and never calls the paid endpoint.
