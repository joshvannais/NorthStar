# Mission 26 Part 5B — exact as-of capacity source gate

Status: source reconciliation only. The released Part 5B diagnostics and guarded current snapshot remain unmounted prerequisites. This note does not issue a capacity forecast, change Mission 22/23 authority, add a route or migration, or accept Part 5B.

## What the current read proves

`src/forecasting/declaredAvailabilitySnapshot.js` reads the current tenant roster, active Business Profile, Mission 22 declared-availability authority and intervals, approved scheduled assignments, and bounded assigned-skill records inside one repeatable-read transaction under Mission 22's current actor and subscription checks. It pins the PostgreSQL MVCC snapshot identity, a digest of the read basis, and an observation time obtained **after** the snapshot is established. Its `sourceAuthenticated: true` means these current records came through that guarded read. `temporalCutoffVerified: false` is intentional. The operating-window bridge can calculate company-open time from that source, but it cannot promote the result into available worker-hours.

An MVCC snapshot ID and later `clock_timestamp()` do not prove a complete historical view at that later wall-clock time. A transaction can commit between snapshot establishment and the observation clock; its rows would be absent although its record time could precede the observation time. Conversely, a transaction's `NOW()` timestamp may precede its commit. Neither timestamp alone certifies when a historical forecast could actually have seen a revision. Do not set `asOf = observedAt` and relabel the current snapshot as an exact historical cutoff.

## Existing history and remaining proof

Mission 22 already has immutable availability revisions with coverage, intervals, digest and `created_at` (`migrations/034_schedule_availability_conflict_authority.sql`), and schedule assignment revisions with approval identity, assignment state, interval and digest (`migrations/032_canonical_schedule_assignment_authority.sql`). Business Profile versions exist in `migrations/005_canonical_organization_authority.sql`. The current reader instead uses active rows through `src/scheduling/conflictRepository.js`; it does not reconstruct every source as it was visible at an arbitrary earlier cutoff. The active workforce roster, memberships, skill associations, profile activation and any corrections must be reconciled at the same cutoff. Mission 23's private work-profile events are not directly readable by the runtime role, so certification evidence needs its own authorized projection if later required. A skill assignment and an approved certificate still do not establish qualification for a particular job or machine.

The Part 2A as-of manifest (`src/forecasting/asOfSourceManifest.js`) validates caller-supplied revision pins, recorded times and identity. It does not authenticate source visibility or completeness. An exact Part 5B adapter must establish a source-owned visibility/receipt policy for every input, select one applicable revision per identity, prove all relevant rows and absences were covered at the cutoff, and reject later corrections leaking backward. It must bind the Business Profile calendar, roster, qualifications, absences, approved commitments and shared resources to the same tenant, cutoff and future horizon. Missing or bounded evidence is unavailable, never zero. Historical backtests must hide records not yet visible at each prediction origin.

## Smallest safe next implementation decision

Keep the current snapshot as a **current descriptive** source. Before any `capacity.available_role_hours.v1` value is issued, add an owning-source adapter that proves exact as-of visibility and completeness for the target's grain. Reuse Mission 22's revision and permission contracts; do not widen private table grants or manufacture a cutoff from timestamps. If existing audit/revision records cannot establish visibility for one required dimension, introduce only an additive, source-owned receipt/projection under the appropriate existing mission authority and keep that dimension unavailable until it has acceptance evidence. Do not relax Part 2A or Part 3 evaluation gates to make an early number appear.

Acceptance must include a real role-separated disposable PostgreSQL run, same-tenant and cross-tenant cases, bounded pagination, an interleaved commit around snapshot capture, correction/revocation after cutoff, profile/roster/schedule changes, DST and overnight hours, and a historical reconstruction that cannot see later facts. Independently validate that no worker is counted twice and that a role or skill association never becomes certification or job-specific eligibility without owning evidence. Paid/demo and saved-run/outcome gates remain separate.
