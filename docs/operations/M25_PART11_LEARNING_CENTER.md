# Mission 25 Part 11 Learning Center

## Owner experience

The paid Learning Center now includes material, inventory and purchasing sources beside labor, travel, vehicle and equipment sources. The isolated demo uses the same presentation contract with illustrative read-only records. Owners and administrators can see:

- whether completed-job material comparisons are allowed;
- imported source permission, record counts and review progress;
- exact job, material, vendor and inventory-location links;
- separate permissions for quantity and waste, cost and purchasing, and planning suggestions;
- independent material-use, waste, unit-cost, purchase-quantity and purchase-cost suggestions;
- unavailable measures with the reason current evidence cannot support them;
- source update settings, retention, deletion progress and legal or audit holds.

Rendered language avoids database identifiers, request identifiers, credentials and implementation terminology. Company-facing target labels pass the accepted Part 10 presentation safety contract. When a target cannot be represented safely and distinctly, the choice stays unavailable.

## Cleanup and recovery

An active legal or audit hold blocks retention and deletion cleanup. Source deletion pauses source use and hides dependent suggestions. Processing a deletion batch requires a separate checkbox that states the irreversible consequence. Cleanup remains bounded to 100 records and records its minimized receipt.

Canceling deletion permits a new source-permission period. It does not revive deleted details, earlier comparisons or prior planning authority. New quantity, cost and planning permissions are required against the current source grant.

## Evidence boundaries

The Learning Center is advisory. It never changes an estimate, material plan, inventory balance, purchase, vendor, schedule, job or company policy. It does not infer a unit conversion, currency conversion, vendor truth, unrecorded cost, missing waste, purchase quantity or current availability. Physical-device review, manual assistive-technology review and the founder's visual verdict remain separate release evidence.
