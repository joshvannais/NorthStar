# Mission 28 Part 11E — owner worklist and prohibited legal/escalation boundary

Status: implementation candidate only. The executable authority is [MISSION_28_PART11E_OWNER_WORKLIST_LEGAL_BOUNDARY_CONTRACT.json](./MISSION_28_PART11E_OWNER_WORKLIST_LEGAL_BOUNDARY_CONTRACT.json). Part 11D is independently accepted and released at source `5204462d60b8dba20a8148152c1ddffb95da2015`, PR #596, live main `fdc05855e76ccb9a430114f99522fe1b4648ca62`, and Railway deployment `21b71cbc-c331-406a-8374-ff5ba98709c6` (`SUCCESS`).

Part 11E freezes a tenant-owner-readable, no-effect projection over exact recorded Part 11A eligibility, Part 11B policy, Part 11C material, Part 11D suppression/rebuild or separately trusted clear resolution, one exact due/aging projection, and one server-resolved active owner/session/membership capability. It defines what an owner could review. It does not create a worklist record, task, approval request, call, notice, message, provider attempt, collection action or external effect.

## Graduated owner review

An exact-current Part 11D suppression selects **pause review** before every aging-based level. An exact-current rebuild selects **rebuild review**. Only a separately trusted exact-current proof that no Part 11D trigger applies may graduate an overdue item: `overdue_1_30` maps to **approval-request review**, `overdue_31_60` maps to **manual-contact review**, and `overdue_61_90` or `overdue_91_plus` maps to **final-supported-notice review**. Pre-due and due-today evidence do not create a Part 11E late-stage projection. These names are fixed owner-review labels, not persisted work, approval, communication or instructions.

Every input binds one tenant, owner actor/session/membership, invoice/version/customer, policy/version, recipient resolution, source set, immutable authoritative head, exact due/aging revision, and current suppression/rebuild or clear resolution. Suppression or rebuild cannot be overridden by aging. A source correction, new head, new aging projection, policy/material change, recipient change, role/session change or currentness change invalidates reuse. Event and observation times never establish precedence. Stable idempotency rejects clone identities and conflicting replay.

## Prohibited boundary

External collection agencies, legal threats, credit reporting, liens, repossession, financing, unsupported fees, write-offs and legal conclusions remain unavailable without separate authority and professional review. Part 11E cannot model, preview, recommend, schedule, persist, send or claim any of them. Fixed labels contain no customer data, amount, free text, threat, fee, deadline, urgency, legal statement or provider payload.

The complete mounted resolver, exact clear-signal authority, durable worklist/task runtime, recipient/channel/provider authority, private-production/live evidence, professional review and founder visual approval remain unavailable and unclaimed. Migration 287 remains latest and migration 288 is absent. Part 11F and later slices remain unimplemented.
