# Mission 26 Part 7C — equipment and travel cost boundary

Mission 24 already owns equipment-only charges, rentals, owned/financed allocations, operating cost categories, travel, vehicle consumption and logistics. `equipmentCostCalculation.js` preserves unknown costs and rejects duplicate operating/fuel/consumables/maintenance categories. `travelCostComposition.js` is the canonical v3 cost-adoption authority that reconciles equipment bundles and travel coverage, deducting the covered travel share rather than charging it twice. Mission 26 must consume an accepted composition, not independently sum a machine rate, fuel, tow vehicle, trailer, operator and trip charges.

The unmounted `src/forecasting/equipmentTravelPositionDiagnostic.js` inspects bounded **claimed** v3 composition amounts. It keeps equipment, gross travel, the recorded overlap deduction, net travel and their combined position separate. An impossible deduction, currency mismatch, duplicate estimate, later record, incompatible composition, or cost greater than the stated complete direct cost is rejected. Missing cost, truncated/revoked coverage, and work spanning an unallocated reporting window withhold amounts. It is a consistency check, not a new equipment or travel calculator. A digest supplied by a caller is only a pin shape, not proof of a persisted approved adoption.

The output always says `sourceAuthenticated: false`, `categoryBreakdownVerified: false`, `laborOverlapAcrossForecastsVerified: false`, `transportAndOnsiteUseSeparated: false`, and `forecastIssued: false`. Equipment cost may already include fuel, maintenance and consumables; travel may include labor and logistics. Neither amount proves a future service/maintenance schedule or that a vehicle is available. Financing cash allocation and economic recovery are different M24 methods and cannot be collapsed into a monthly installment. A road trip towing equipment and that machine's later onsite productive/travel hours remain different usage. This diagnostic cannot assert those category distinctions from an aggregate receipt.

An owning adapter must authenticate one current/adopted v3 revision and coverage decision per estimate as visible at a common as-of cutoff, with role and tenant isolation, source freshness, approved planned work/window allocation, current assets, shared crew/equipment constraints, and cost-category lineage before a forecast or complete operating-cost rollup. Any future category projection must preserve M24's inclusion/overlap manifest and avoid charging travel labor again beside Part 7A labor. Repeated onsite movement and the execution plan belong to the founder-approved Mission 32 authority; Mission 26 later forecasts only from compatible approved plans and observed outcomes. Asset inventory and maintenance records remain owned by their source systems. No sealed M24/M25 contract, migration, route, provider, customer price or scheduling authority is changed here.
