# Mission 26 Part 5A — approved workload and remaining-hours boundary

Status: bounded, unmounted descriptive candidate. No Mission 22/23 source reader, paid forecast, future capacity claim or automatic schedule action is active in this slice.

Part 3A defines accepted actual person-hours as a historical event flow and remaining approved person-hours as an end-of-horizon stock. These are not interchangeable. Part 5A begins with a smaller present-state question: at one cutoff, how much **approved, unfinished** work remains in accepted scheduled versus approved unscheduled work? Its input must eventually come from guarded, same-tenant owning records. A lead, appointment preference, proposed assignment or preliminary estimate is not approved work.

The pure `workloadPositionDiagnostic` accepts a bounded set of one current revision per work identity, an exact cutoff and a caller-provided coverage claim. It sums exact integer **person-minutes** and keeps scheduled and unscheduled totals separate. Accepted complete work contributes no remaining workload. An in-progress job requires a reviewed remaining-work basis; subtracting arbitrary recorded time from the original plan is not safe when scope and productivity can change. An unknown approval, schedule, progress or remaining-hours basis makes the entire total unavailable. Incomplete, revoked, paginated or off-cutoff coverage does the same. Empty results become zero only under a complete same-cutoff claim. A 256-record cap prevents silently treating a truncated large workspace as complete; a future source adapter must use bounded paging or a separately proven complete aggregate.

The diagnostic validates input shape and arithmetic but **cannot authenticate the claim** that these rows are current, approved, complete or tenant-authorized. It returns `sourceAuthenticated: false` and `forecastIssued: false` even with a numerical result. Before mounting it, a Mission 22/23 reader must prove approved-work identity, current schedule/revision, accepted completion and reopening, remaining person-hour basis, correction history, exact cutoff, entitlement, tenant isolation and pagination completeness. Any conflicting work-to-appointment link remains unavailable. A future workload forecast additionally needs a versioned method, source snapshot, historical outcome comparison and Part 3 evaluation gates; current backlog is not a prediction of future work or capacity.

Mission 20 owns roles and worker availability. Mission 22 owns assignments and scheduling. Mission 23 owns accepted work/time/completion evidence. Mission 24 owns estimate plans and approved customer prices. Mission 25 may provide reviewed contractor-specific outcomes. Part 5B will combine compatible workload with qualified availability, travel, assets and commitments.
