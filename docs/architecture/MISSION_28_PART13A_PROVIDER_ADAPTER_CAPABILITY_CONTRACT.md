## Closed discovery result

Discovery covers exactly eight ordered dimensions: outbound channels, callbacks, attachments, templates, limits, modes, costs and status meanings.

- Authenticated in-app passive state uses no provider and remains internal/no-effect.
- Native handoff remains manual and excluded; it is not opened or counted as an attempt.
- Email, SMS and secure-link delivery adapter families are disabled and unavailable.
- Callback authentication, account/endpoint binding, versions and replay protection are unavailable.
- Provider attachments and provider templates are unavailable. Part 7C internal previews and Part 7E future PDF vocabulary are not provider support.
- Provider limits, quotas, retry limits and costs are unknown and unavailable. Unknown never means zero, free, included, unlimited or documented support. Current accepted external recipients, messages, provider attempts, retries and attachments remain zero.
- `provider_disabled` is the only accepted current external mode. Sandbox and live modes remain unavailable.
- Part 7F status meanings remain a closed vocabulary only. They do not establish a callback, attempt, acceptance, send, delivery, open, reply, opt-out, failure, complaint, correction or business outcome.

The only owner-readable result is `unavailable_all_external_provider_adapters_disabled` with the fixed label “External provider channels are unavailable.” Candidate data cannot create or replace the separately trusted disabled registry resolution.
