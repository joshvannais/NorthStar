# Mission 28 Part 8D — Delivery acknowledgement, recovery and exact labels

Status: **Implementation candidate only.** This contract creates no send, delivery acknowledgement, view, reply, revoke, resend, recovery action, customer outcome or external effect.

## Exact presentation, not manufactured evidence

Every label is fixed catalog copy derived only from separately trusted immutable evidence. A queue row, preview, resolution, native composer opening or provider acceptance never means delivered. Sent never means delivered; delivered never means viewed; viewed never means replied; and a reply or question never means a revision was accepted, a balance was paid, or a business outcome occurred. Unknown, conflicting, corrected, late or partial evidence fails closed to a reconciliation-required label and blocks resend.

Current Part 8C creates no durable delivery intent, provider request identity, message, native handoff or external effect. Part 7F has no current provider or recipient evidence, and no Part 6F recovery assessment can be bound to a nonexistent Part 8C action unit. The only current delivery label is therefore **Not sent — delivery unavailable**. Status read is a fixed no-effect projection; revoke, resend, recovery, customer-response read and inaccurate-recipient correction return fixed unavailable or not-applicable labels.

Reserved future labels do not grant future authority. They may be selected only after the exact Part 7F evidence prerequisite and any later accepted provider, callback or authenticated inbound boundary exist. No candidate, provider payload, path, recipient value or free text is reflected into a label.

## Revoke, resend, recovery and corrections

A revoke request is an internal decision, never proof that a provider, recipient or effect was recalled, stopped or deleted. Resend may not reuse or relabel a Part 8C request, stable idempotency identity, provider request identity or uncertain outcome. A future resend requires separately accepted reviewed-resend authority, exact reconciliation, new exact-current Parts 7A–8C material and authorization, and one stable new provider request identity. Those prerequisites are unavailable.

Part 6F recovery remains immutable no-effect evidence. It cannot restart, reclaim, resend or create provider authority. A customer question, revision request or inaccurate-recipient correction requires future authenticated inbound or exact-current recipient evidence and a new reviewed source, preview, permission and authorization. Earlier request, delivery, correction, revoke and recovery history is append-only and never retargeted.
