# Mission 25 Part 13E proposal registry and impact preview

Part 13E turns one exact current Part 13D proposal into an owner-readable impact preview. Labor, travel, vehicle and equipment, materials, scope and connected financial evidence remain separate. Supported multiplier advice is expressed only as a relative increase, decrease or keep review. A connected gross-margin percentage remains reference-only. Absolute company values stay unavailable because the accepted evidence does not establish them, and native NorthStar financial truth remains a Mission 27 dependency.

An owner or administrator may explicitly save the preview as an immutable registry revision for the normalized service. Every revision pins the current Part 13D permission period, exact proposal identity and canonical and source digests, exact preview and preview digest, previous registry revision, deterministic calculation version, actor authority and request identity. Exact current retries are idempotent; a newer registry revision retires an earlier replay.

The preview and registry are advisory. Saving a version changes no business profile, planning value, estimate, price, schedule, job, asset, material, financial record or company policy. Part 13F remains the only future slice authorized to apply an owner-approved proposal through the authority that owns the affected value.

Any pinned proposal, graph, evaluation, summary, source, target or permission change makes a registry version stale and masks its proposal identity and preview. Revocation hides the current period. A later permission grant starts an empty registry period and cannot revive earlier versions.

Runtime can execute only the guarded impact-preview, registry-save and registry-read entries. It cannot read registry storage or execute validation, lineage, projection or trigger helpers. This slice adds no rendered page. Adoption, lifecycle propagation and the paid/demo experience remain Parts 13F-H.
