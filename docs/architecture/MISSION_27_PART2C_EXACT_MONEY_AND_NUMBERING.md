## Authority

Part 2C implements the frozen exact-money and tenant document-numbering contract. Launch currency is USD with exponent two. Durable money crosses the application boundary as canonical base-10 minor-unit strings and an explicit currency; JavaScript `Number` arithmetic, implicit zero, implicit foreign exchange, and silently rounded caller input are rejected. Exact zero remains a proved value. A later calculation owner may apply the frozen half-away-from-zero rule at its own explicit boundary, but this slice does not invent a tax, price, allocation, invoice, payment, refund, or revenue amount.

The reviewed numbering contract is tenant-global, location-independent, server-assigned and versioned. Invoice, credit, and receipt sequences are separate, monotonic, ten digits wide, and prefixed `INV-`, `CR-`, and `RCT-`. They begin at one, never reset annually, permit gaps, and never reuse a durable reservation. The policy explicitly makes no statutory-sufficiency claim. The frozen launch machine policy now classifies the distinct owner-only `customer_financial.document_numbering.review` capability as `single_control` with `current_session` assurance under `m27.owner.v1`. Only a current paid owner with that conformingly issued capability, tenant-numbering target, `customer_financial.document_numbering` purpose, `mission27.launch.v1` policy, CSRF, reason, and idempotency evidence may review the fixed launch contract. This capability is narrower than the released source-fact, billing-identity, draft, or invoice-action capabilities and grants none of them. Administrators and role-only callers cannot review this owner business setting.

## Durable contract

Migration `262_customer_financial_exact_money_numbering_v1.sql` adds private capability-grant/head, reviewed-policy/request, sequence, reservation, and lifecycle-event relations. Reviewed policies, requests, reservations, and events are append-only. The sequence head is the only mutable numbering row and has no runtime table privilege. Runtime may execute only the guarded policy candidate, read, and review entries. Exact-money SQL normalization, contract construction, reservation, lifecycle transition, and recovery primitives are withheld from both the runtime role and `PUBLIC` until an owning later slice wraps them in its separately accepted action authority.

Reservation is atomic per tenant and document class. A reservation binds one policy ID and digest, exact class, numeric sequence, rendered number, request-key digest, request digest, and trusted time. Exact reservation and lifecycle-event replay returns the original event receipt even after later transitions; changed reuse conflicts. Recovery reads at most that exact tenant/request pair and returns its latest state. Issued and void states append events to the reservation and cannot change its number. The private lifecycle primitive accepts only `reserved → issued`, `reserved → void`, or `issued → void`; an issued-to-void transition must retain the exact document identity and digest. A void number remains consumed. These state primitives do not create, issue, correct, or void a financial document.

## Access, concurrency and recovery

Trusted numbering-grant issuance first ratifies the capability against the frozen owner template and exhaustive `actionControl` machine map, then persists the exact template, action mode and assurance in the private immutable grant. Missing, administrator, member, mismatched-template, mismatched-mode or mismatched-assurance issuance is denied before insertion. Policy review serializes the request key and tenant policy, rechecks paid entitlement and the exact grant at effect time, and stores its mode/assurance proof plus a minimized receipt for every accepted key. Grant issuance and policy/request insertion share one logical capability lock; a superseding revision that wins leaves no obsolete policy or receipt. Cancellation that wins the subscription lock leaves no residue. The fixed policy digest must match before any effect, so a stale client cannot acknowledge a changed contract.

Number allocation uses the database as the sole arithmetic and sequencing authority. Concurrent distinct requests receive unique increasing numbers. Concurrent duplicates serialize on the hashed request key. The recovery lookup cannot search by prefix, number, actor claim, document claim, or another tenant. Sequence exhaustion fails closed. A transaction that never commits created no durable reservation; after a committed but uncertain response, the same key recovers the exact durable number. No abandoned, issued, or void durable reservation is reallocated.
