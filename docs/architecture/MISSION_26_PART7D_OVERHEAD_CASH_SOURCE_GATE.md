# Mission 26 Part 7D - overhead and financed-asset cash forecast

Migration 243 closes the previously documented source-authority gap with the smallest additive owner-recorded source. `canonical_operating_cost_schedule_revisions` is an append-only, tenant-private complete schedule snapshot owned by Business Profile and asset operations. Mission 26 cannot write it through a forecast route. An owner or administrator privately reads, records, corrects or revokes the source through the guarded Business Profile schedule endpoint; mutations require CSRF, idempotency, expected revision/digest and an exact allocation policy. The runtime role has no table privileges and can use only the guarded source read/mutation and aggregate forecast entries.

Each active snapshot pins one currency, effective date, inclusive coverage dates, recorded-through instant, complete-coverage attestation and current revision. Every schedule has a stable source key, exact constant amount rule, explicit due dates, recurrence end, payment status, included categories and either an owner attestation or a source-document digest. A financed-asset obligation must reference one current tenant asset and explicitly include debt service. Overhead schedules cannot disguise principal, interest or debt service as overhead. Corrections replace the complete set; revocation makes the forecast unavailable. A complete attested empty set is the only way zero can issue.

The policy keeps Mission 24 and Mission 26 meanings separate. Mission 24's job-specific overhead allocation, equipment economic recovery and financing-cash allocation remain estimate economics. Migration 243 requires the owner to mark those allocations separate from the dated company schedule. The Part 7D read never adds a Mission 24 pool, assumes an installment from asset ownership, or treats economic depreciation as cash. It sums only due dates marked `scheduled` inside the next 30 days. `owner_marked_satisfied` and canceled dates are excluded from future commitments, but the output explicitly says a schedule is not proof that payment occurred.

`GET /api/v1/forecast/overhead-cash/current` is a private owner/admin, no-store, read-only projection. It fails closed when the Business Profile currency or time zone is missing, the complete owner-recorded source is absent/revoked/stale, coverage does not span the local 30-day horizon, currency conflicts, a referenced asset is missing/archived, or the allocation reconciliation is no longer current. The response exposes only aggregate overhead and financed-asset cash amounts and counts. It does not expose source keys, asset IDs, labels, documents, digests or private schedule detail.

Mission 25 outcome learning does not rewrite contractual due dates or amounts. No learned adjustment is applied. External financial observations remain retrospective context and cannot supply a forward obligation schedule. The forecast also does not claim off-platform completeness, actual payment, recognized expense, economic depreciation, a complete operating-cost forecast, probability, calibrated range or an automatic action.

The existing Command Center **Cost & operational risk** detail gains one compact row and an atomic polite status. The isolated demo uses a fixed fictional local snapshot and never calls the paid endpoint. Focused contract, PostgreSQL, API and browser checks cover exact positive and authenticated zero values, correction/revocation and stale coverage, role/tenant/asset isolation, currency and category conflicts, owner-marked satisfied versus actual payment, paid/demo separation, loading/unavailable/recovery and responsive rendering. Independent exact-head audit and the normal release gate remain separate acceptance evidence.
