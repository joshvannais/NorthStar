# Mission 28 Part 13E — privacy lifecycle boundary

Status: **Implementation candidate only. Not independently accepted or released.**

Part 13E freezes a tenant-private, provider-neutral grammar for privacy-safe metrics and traces, immutable action audit, retention and deletion review, legal/audit hold review, customer erasure review, tenant offboarding review, policy/source revocation review, minimized export, cleanup review, incident evidence, and recovery evidence. It mounts no resolver, export, deletion, erasure, cleanup, offboarding, revocation, provider, message, or effect path. Every current inspection returns the fixed nonreflective label `Privacy lifecycle operations are unavailable.`

## Exact boundaries

- Metrics and traces are limited to one exact tenant and fixed dimensions, bounded counts/duration buckets, internal opaque identities, canonical digests, sequences, revisions, fixed states/reason codes, trusted timestamps, capabilities, and record scopes. The complete fixed privacy profile is bound by the exact domain-separated digest `sha256:ad76777d0711a2c5f48f8983fc00d7fec4302980930a560c5ad6e567f44d78b5`; an arbitrary, stale, or content-incoherent profile digest fails closed. Message bodies, document or attachment content, credentials, secrets, signatures, authorization headers, public tokens, addresses, customer contact values, free text, provider/callback payloads, IP addresses, user agents, and unnecessary customer data are forbidden.
- Platform-wide or cross-tenant analytics are not Part 13E authority. They remain Mission 33 and require separate privacy authority.
- Action audit is append-only. Lifecycle review cannot rewrite or delete an immutable action, historical run binding, policy/source lineage, provider evidence, financial/accounting fact, or upstream business fact.
- Retention duration, automatic deletion, hold creation/release, customer identity verification and erasure, tenant offboarding, provider disconnect, export, cleanup, and revocation execution remain unavailable. The operation matrix is exact: destructive deletion, erasure, cleanup, and offboarding cleanup require reviewed deletion eligibility plus exact hold clearance; `reviewed_retain` blocks them. Retention review requires exact retention evidence, and hold review requires exact hold evidence. Nondestructive metric/trace/audit/export/incident/recovery reads and safety revocation reviews neither require nor infer destructive lifecycle authority.
- An active or unknown hold blocks only deletion, erasure cleanup, general cleanup, and offboarding cleanup. It does not block the nondestructive or safety-review operations above. This is a data-preservation rule, not a legal conclusion. Professional legal/privacy/regulatory review is unavailable and unclaimed.
- Customer erasure is exact tenant/customer scope only and requires separately trusted latest-current customer-identity-verification and source-ownership-map lineages with exact tenant, identity, digest, sequence, predecessor, currentness, and fixed state. It cannot silently delete shared records, another customer's records, immutable audit, financial/accounting truth, or upstream facts, and it cannot claim an upstream system erased anything.
- Tenant offboarding binds stop, policy/source revocation, minimized export, retention/hold, provider disconnect, cleanup, and completion as seven separately trusted latest-current lineages. Each has an exact tenant, lineage, head, predecessor, sequence, currentness, and closed state. No component is inferred from another; provider disconnect, cleanup, and completion remain explicitly unavailable and no completion is claimed.
- Policy/source revocation may stop future eligibility only after later accepted authority; it preserves historical bindings and never rewrites history or means the upstream source was deleted.
- Export requires the released `automation_evidence_export` capability, recent authentication, exact tenant scope, fixed allowlist, bounds, and cursor identity. The executor is unavailable.
- Incident and recovery evidence is minimized and never proves a provider outcome, effect, legal finding, or successful recovery. Part 6F's no-effect recovery boundary remains controlling.

The executable JSON freezes exact shapes, operation-specific authority/disposition rules, bounded component lineages, the domain-separated privacy-profile digest, closed operations, precedence, digest/idempotency rules, a single-authoritative-successor lineage, fixed unavailable result, denied combinations, and all evidence that remains unavailable. Assessment hashes are SHA-256 lowercase hexadecimal over UTF-8 JSON after recursively sorting object keys, preserving array order, and retaining JSON primitive encoding; the executable validator pins this exact declaration and a fixed known vector. Candidate data cannot create trusted retention, hold, erasure, offboarding, revocation, export, cleanup, incident, recovery, provider, effect, financial, money, or legal authority.

Migration 287 remains latest and migration 288 is absent. Founder visual approval, private-production lifecycle evidence, provider/live evidence, professional review, and Mission 33 analytics authority remain separate and unavailable.
