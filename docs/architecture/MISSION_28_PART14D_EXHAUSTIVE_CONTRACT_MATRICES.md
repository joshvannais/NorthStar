## Matrix contract

The executable matrix enumerates all frozen axes: policies; sources; versions;
digests; currentness; roles and capabilities; sessions and tenants; recipients
and consent; paths, queries, bodies, framing, methods and CORS; templates and
callbacks; idempotency, concurrency, corrections, retries and restarts;
privacy; and zero unintended effects.

Semantic cases execute the mounted shared business-decision engine. An exact,
current, completely bound no-effect input is the positive control. Each
restrictive policy, source, recipient, consent, suppression, schedule,
execution or recovery mutation must become unavailable, ineligible, blocked,
expired, or recovery-bound as the accepted engine specifies. Missing or mixed
authority never becomes eligible, scheduled, clear, ready, successful or zero.
Component and envelope digests bind exact tenant, session-owned fictional
identity, policy/source versions, action, recipient, channel, schedule and
execution material. Cross-tenant or cross-session retargeting is rejected.

Transport cases exercise the paid and isolated-demo routers. Paid reads remain
tenant-authenticated, capability-scoped and owner/admin only. Paid previews
require mutation/session authority and remain pure. Demo reads reject query
authority. Demo previews and actions require same-origin framing, an exact
explicit intent, closed bodies, current workspace revision and the existing
tenant/session-bound fictional workspace. Actions additionally retain stable
idempotency and compare-and-swap. Methods, paths, queries, bodies, framing and
CORS cannot create business authority. Responses remain no-store and do not
reflect secrets, credentials, private payloads or unnecessary customer data.

Corrections rebuild current authority instead of reviving stale evidence.
Retries do not create duplicate effects. Stale concurrency loses to the
current revision. Restart and real PostgreSQL durability claims remain
explicitly unavailable until Part 14E instead of being inferred from unit
matrices. Templates are a closed owner-readable catalog. Callback state is
only the previously accepted authenticated reconciliation contract; Part 14D
does not simulate a provider callback or strengthen provider evidence.

Every matrix records zero real recipients, provider calls, paid-demo
cross-adoption, external effects, financial effects and money effects. Live or
sandbox providers, private-production customer evidence, professional review,
genuine Chrome/WebKit acceptance, physical devices, manual assistive
technology, founder visual approval, independent Part 14D audit, PR, merge,
deployment and production acceptance remain unavailable and unclaimed.
