# Mission 26 Part 5B constrained capacity candidate boundary

This candidate implements the complete scoped Part 5B research authority for `capacity.available_role_hours.v1`. It measures role-worker hours over one server-issued, half-open 2,592,000-second future horizon. It never caps supply by backlog: one hour of approved work does not turn one hundred otherwise available role-worker hours into one hour of capacity. Exact approved scheduled commitments and their reviewed travel buffers consume declared supply; unscheduled work without a commitment does not.

Each tenant-private scope names one declared operational role and explicitly marks all seven required dimensions applicable or not applicable: crew formation, internal skill associations, Business Profile working hours, exact locations, owner-reviewed estimated or reported travel pairs, vehicle suitability and equipment suitability. Crew-applicable scopes may require arbitrary bounded target and support seats; crew-not-applicable scopes retain the same role-worker-hour target without inventing a crew. The scope pins a bounded, ordered worker, crew, skill, profile, asset, capability and asset-calendar identity. Its reviewed crew and asset assignments are eligibility and formation inputs to the private deterministic calculation; they are not assignments, dispatches or reservations, and they cannot replace complete server-selected matching. Separate job reviews cover every server-selected approved unfinished assignment and bind its exact assignment profile or crew, compatible role, opportunity, exact estimate population, scope, locations and asset requirements. A complete approved-work census is established before enrichment.

The registered measure remains role-worker hours. The purpose-fixed method composes target and supporting roles per half-open source interval. Every worker in the complete base-role population must appear in the reviewed scope, so callers cannot cherry-pick people. A worker in multiple listed crews is counted once. Each interval intersects Business Profile working hours, reviewed declared worker availability, genuine direct and crew commitments, current crew membership and internal skill association, reviewed operator eligibility, exact vehicle and equipment category/configuration/home location, service capability, declared asset availability and asset commitments. A named asset operator must itself be a qualified, available formation member in a compatible reviewed role; operators and assets cannot be substituted or reused at the same instant. The solver counts the maximum complete formation it can establish without reusing a person or asset at that instant. A scheduled commitment must belong to the scope's exact worker or crew. Ambiguous job-to-scope mapping, incomplete crew formation, unavailable operator, asset conflict or unsupported transition refuses the whole receipt instead of invoking a heuristic.

Scopes carry an exact alternative key. People, crews and assets may appear in separate alternatives because each alternative is a distinct output and is never summed with another. Within one alternative, overlapping scopes that would reuse any effective reviewed person refuse before persistence, including direct profiles, direct-versus-crew members, support roles and operators; crew and asset overlap refuses as well. Every approved work identity must have exactly one reviewed job mapping in every alternative, so an alternative cannot silently omit work or borrow another alternative's manifest.

Scheduled work is ordered separately for each exact assignment profile or crew route. The first commitment on each route must begin from that route's reviewed home, each next commitment must name the prior job location, the preceding job must name that next location, and the last commitment must return to the same home. Each unique leg needs one reviewed travel pair and enough elapsed time between work intervals; it is deducted once for the exact target-role seat count. Simultaneous disjoint routes remain distinct. Missing, duplicated, contradictory or overlapping route evidence refuses the whole receipt rather than double counting a leg or inferring a road route.

All constraint meanings are narrow installed-source evidence. Mission 23 approved profiles and reviewed certifications, Mission 22 declared availability and approved commitments, workforce crew and skill records, Business Profile calendars, tenant asset configuration, Mission 23 equipment ledgers and adopted Mission 24 equipment, readiness and travel records remain owned by their existing authorities. Each job explicitly identifies equipment, readiness and travel as not applicable, as a Part 5B owner-reviewed internal basis, or as an exact current adopted Mission 24 receipt. An M24 claim pins its receipt UUID and digest and refuses if the current owning receipt differs; there is no implicit adoption. Part 5B adds an append-only owner/admin human review of exact applicability, internal operational suitability, future asset calendar and travel estimate. Those decisions do not prove independent credentials, realized attendance, physical asset availability, provider-verified road routes or reservations.

Every participating source mutation advances one tenant-private generation row before the owning row changes and writes its ordered observation in the same transaction. Guarded reads and writes lock that row with the source tables. A writer already in flight therefore commits before the guarded statement can use evidence and forces a serialization conflict if its transaction snapshot predates that generation; a writer that starts later waits until the guarded transaction finishes. The fence and event ledger are private helpers with no runtime or `PUBLIC` table access.

The lifecycle is prospective. A paid owner or admin explicitly records the purpose-fixed method, scope and every applicable job review, then records a coverage epoch. Capture locks and rechecks the genuine source authorities and current subscription, session and access before deriving the server cutoff. An immutable origin pins exact source, review, census, calculation and private result identities. Supported ordinary source evolution strictly inside the future horizon is reconstructed from owning occurrence plus private observation order and integrated across the whole period; it does not rewrite the origin. Mission 22 schedule revisions form human-approved authority intervals: a scheduled revision consumes only the intersection of its approved schedule and its own authority interval, a later unschedule stops future consumption, and a later reschedule begins a distinct commitment without moving or erasing the elapsed interval. The later outcome reconstructs the original half-open period's complete approved-work population, including work created during the period and commitments that later completed or were cancelled, rather than substituting the next window's backlog. Required human reviews become their own interval boundaries. A pre-origin correction or a transition whose effective history cannot be proved makes current use stale or refuses rather than inventing retroactive occurrence. Activity at the inclusive start is applicable authority; activity at the exclusive end belongs to the next period, and ordinary post-end progress does not rewrite ended evidence. A separate post-horizon outcome and evaluation are append-only. A correction to a pinned completion or cancellation makes the old outcome and evaluation stale and requires an explicit new revision. A new epoch permanently retires older receipts and grants no automatic approval.

The paid HTTP projection is deliberately nonnumeric. It exposes receipt identity, fixed horizon, scope count, current/stale state and mandatory false serving/action flags. Role-hour results, source manifests, worker/job/asset identities, digests and evaluation metrics stay private. Reads perform no source or forecast mutation. Missing, conflicting, revoked, oversized, overlapping or partially classified evidence refuses the whole write; authenticated complete zero remains distinct from missing evidence. Runtime receives only guarded entry execution, while private tables, sequence, helpers and triggers remain denied and startup verifies exact security and trigger identity.

This package does not perform Part 5C advice or Part 5D UI. It does not schedule, assign, dispatch, reserve assets, alter estimates, hire, certify workers or take commercial action. Provider, off-platform, legacy, backfilled, unsaved and whole-business completeness; natural production history; empirical accuracy, calibration, drift or statistical confidence; verified travel, credential, attendance or physical asset conclusions; public or paid numeric forecast serving; automatic action; WebKit and physical-device evidence; complete accessibility; and founder visual approval remain unavailable.

This is a candidate implementation record, not canonical acceptance. Part 5B remains incomplete until the frozen exact code head passes a genuinely independent whole-slice audit, merges, deploys with production health verification, and a separate canonical post-release acceptance refresh completes its own audit, merge and deployment gates. Part 5C and later slices remain paused.
