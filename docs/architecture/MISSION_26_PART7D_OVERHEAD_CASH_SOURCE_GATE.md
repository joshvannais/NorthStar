# Mission 26 Part 7D — overhead and financed-asset cash source gate

Status: source reconciliation only. This does not issue an overhead or cash-commitment forecast, add a financial ledger, alter Mission 24/25, create a route or migration, or accept Part 7D.

## What the released foundations actually contain

Mission 24's `src/estimating/pricingCalculation.js` supports **job-specific overhead allocation** as a fixed amount, percentage, or finite period pool with job and total allocation units. Its `coverage.included` references subtract reviewed amounts already carried elsewhere; the same expense cannot be charged again as incremental overhead. This establishes a possible commercial cost basis for an estimate. It is not a record of every company expense paid during each calendar month. `src/services/businessProfileAdapter.js` can carry an explicit `canonicalCosts.overheadPercent`, but a percentage is not an actual dated expense or a sufficient source for monthly operating cash.

Mission 24's `src/estimating/equipmentCostCalculation.js` distinguishes `economic_recovery` from `financing_cash` equipment allocation and rejects capital-recovery/debt-service overlap. The period pool and usable equipment hours are a **job-cost allocation method**. They do not identify a lender, exact contractual due dates, each payment amount, remaining balance, payoff, or whether a particular financed asset has an installment in a forecast month. `migrations/016_tenant_asset_catalogue.sql` explicitly excludes asset costs, and its asset identity record has no financing schedule. There is no basis to assume every job or asset owes a monthly installment.

Mission 25's consented external financial import and per-estimate advice can supply reviewed context under the [Part 6C financial boundary](MISSION_26_PART6C_FINANCIAL_EVIDENCE_COMPATIBILITY.md). An accounting cost observation is not automatically a complete forward contractual schedule. Mission 27's native invoice/payment lifecycle concerns customer billing and collections; it does not by itself create asset-debt obligations.

## Minimum compatible future source

Part 7D can consume a source-owned, owner-recorded **obligation schedule** only when that source has explicit tenant/asset or business-expense identity, currency, amount or exact amount rule, due dates and recurrence end, payment status, source document/attestation, effective revision and correction/revocation, as-of visibility, complete coverage and owner/role authorization. A financed asset may have zero, one or several distinct obligations; ownership, financing and expected utilization must remain separate. The source must state whether an amount includes interest, principal, insurance, tax, maintenance or another charge so Mission 24 equipment pools and overhead allocation do not count it again. Availability of a source record is not evidence that an installment was actually paid. A repayment schedule is a cash-commitment projection, not economic depreciation or recognized expense.

Overhead forecasts likewise need a defined periodic expense source or approved allocation policy, with applicability, included-cost references, changes and calendar basis. A single overhead percentage may support a stated **job-cost scenario** when its direct-cost basis is known, but cannot be presented as verified monthly fixed overhead or cash outflow. Missing obligations, incomplete expense coverage, expired policy and unresolved category overlap remain **unavailable**, never zero. The Part 7A/B/C positions are unmounted, claimed-input prerequisites and must not be aggregated into a paid profit forecast yet.

Mission 26 Part 7D owns forecast consumption and the distinction between economic cost and dated cash commitment. The Business Profile/asset source write authority is not established by the released records inspected above. Mission 20 owns contractor Business Profile and asset identity; adding a financing schedule to that sealed authority is not implied here. Mission 33 is explicitly the **NorthStar platform-operator** control plane, not a contractor financing ledger. If no approved future authority specifies the schedule record and its permissions, this is an **unowned source-authority gap for founder review**, not a reason to invent payments inside Mission 26 or reopen sealed Mission 24/25.

Acceptance requires exact owner-recorded schedule fixtures and an authenticated, tenant-isolated source reader; due-date recurrence and changes; period/time-zone and currency handling; zero versus missing; cancellation/payoff and correction at historical cutoffs; reconciliation against M24 equipment pool and overhead-included references; paid/demo isolation; independent PostgreSQL, API and UI review; and later observed-vs-predicted evaluation. Until then Part 7D stays open and no cash-commitment or complete operating-profit forecast may issue.
