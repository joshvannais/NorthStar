# Mission 26 Part 8C — declared route-load prerequisite

The unmounted `src/forecasting/routeLoadPositionDiagnostic.js` reuses the sealed Mission 24 Travel & Logistics calculator to validate each submitted travel plan and obtain its trip-leg and vehicle-leg counts. It then multiplies each declared one-way distance by the corresponding canonical vehicle-leg count. Miles and kilometres remain separate; it does not silently convert or combine them. A return leg is counted only when Mission 24's recorded trip says it is included, and asymmetric routes remain separate lines. Straight-line geometry, unknown distance, an incomplete or revoked claimed set, truncation, or a work window outside the horizon withholds the whole footprint. It rejects duplicate estimate identities, tenant mismatch, future records and unsafe nested inputs.

The output is **declared vehicle-leg distance**, not independently verified road mileage. Mission 24 travel lines do not currently classify transportation to a site versus onsite equipment movement; the diagnostic therefore keeps `roadAndOnsiteSeparated: false`. It does not infer fuel or energy usage from a monetary vehicle charge, calculate logistics capacity, prove road routing, verify provider distance, reserve a vehicle, or issue a forecast. The caller's adoption, coverage, source digest and work dates are not authenticated by this pure function. The separate Mission 26 Part 7C composition diagnostic remains the cost-overlap boundary; this quantity check neither recomposes costs nor charges travel labor. Mission 32 owns future execution-plan and internal jobsite-movement authority.

Final Part 8C acceptance needs guarded, complete as-of adopted travel-plan sources; explicit movement class and resource identity; applicable source-aware distance/routing and capacity; independently sourced fuel or energy consumption and inclusion rules; long-job phase attribution; corrections and cancellation propagation; calibrated outcome evidence; paid/demo recovery; and independent end-to-end review. Missing data remains unavailable. This prerequisite adds no route, migration or UI and does not complete Part 8C.
