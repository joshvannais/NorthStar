## Authority and calculation

`estimate-pricing-plan-v1` is independent of human scope/price decisions and of the four cost components. Saving it never changes either. The current selected original/child estimate, global decision basis, previous pricing receipt and current source digest bind each attempt. Component adoption makes an earlier proposal visibly historical and requires a fresh calculation/acknowledgment. No private pricing value replaces original-source summary amounts or the human-approved price in CAPELLA.

At most 12 charge lines support fixed service charges, measured unit charges, packages with explicit included-line identities, and finite whole-period retainers. The inclusion graph has one parent per included line and no cycles; included prices are displayed but added only once. Retainer dates describe the scope; they do not infer proration, renewal or number of periods. Each unit/retainer line rounds half up once to cents. Money has two decimal places, quantities/rates at most four; bounded integer/numeric arithmetic is shared between JavaScript and PostgreSQL. Known zero differs from missing.

Up to 12 payment stages divide the proposed before-tax charge. There is at most one deposit and one final balance. Amount entries must sum exactly; percentage shares sum to 100%, each nonfinal amount floors to cents, and the explicit final balance receives the rounding residual. These are proposed payment terms, not extra fees or recorded payments.

Overhead may remain unknown, be a fixed job allocation, a percentage of complete recorded direct costs, or a declared share of a dated expense pool using matching person-hours/jobs. No utilization is inferred. A user explicitly declares separation from recorded costs or identifies bounded amounts already included. Adopted labor task totals provide maximum capacities for an owner's declared overhead share; they do not verify the internal expense split. Unknown overlap keeps complete costs unavailable; duplicate references/overclaims reject. Equipment/travel/field-labor costs are not added a second time. The SQL basis retains original and v1/v2/v3 component and coverage semantics; it does not reprice old records.

Owner estimates require no fabricated dates or reference. Current profile and published internal financial/service knowledge may be selected deliberately; a reference does not verify a selling price. Current source availability, privacy and publication filtering remain authoritative. Unknown/future/expired dates are visible cautions. One server UTC date is bound to the source digest; a midnight boundary requires a fresh calculation. Saved evidence retains its review date while current read cautions can change. Withdraw remains available after source revocation. Historical stored evidence is immutable, but unavailable source content is redacted from current/history projections. Mutation responses expose receipt identities only; replay cannot expose revoked content.
