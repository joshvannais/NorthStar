# Mission 26 Part 5B — declared role-time position

Status: pure, unmounted arithmetic candidate. It is neither authenticated workforce evidence nor a capacity forecast. No route, migration, schedule, assignment, asset reservation, user-facing value or forecast run is added.

`src/forecasting/roleTimePositionDiagnostic.js` answers one smaller question for a declared role and a future UTC window: if the supplied roster and interval evidence were complete and current at the cutoff, how many **declared, uncommitted qualified person-minutes** would remain? It unions overlapping working and declared-available intervals, intersects those two sets, subtracts declared unavailability and approved commitments, and clips the result to the window. It does not turn roster size, business hours or a qualification label into assumed free time. The result remains `sourceAuthenticated: false`, `forecastIssued: false` and `resourceConstraintsChecked: false` even when arithmetic returns a number.

The input requires a versioned role key, same-tenant worker identities and record pins, exact cutoff and future window, a source-snapshot digest, complete-coverage claim and bounded minute-aligned UTC intervals. Missing, revoked, paginated or off-cutoff coverage and unknown worker or qualification state withhold the entire total. Empty roster means zero only under an exact complete-coverage claim. The caller cannot prove its own coverage, role qualification, employment state, interval provenance or source digest simply by supplying this shape. One employee may be qualified for multiple roles; role-specific results must not be summed as independent people without an allocation rule.

A future Mission 20/22 owning-source adapter must establish current active workforce and authoritative qualifications, correct local calendar/time-zone conversion, declared availability and absence coverage, approved overlapping commitments, current schedule/crew revisions, tenant/role access, exact as-of visibility and complete bounded pagination. Mission 22's existing conflict and scheduling evaluators remain authoritative for assignment feasibility; this diagnostic does not recreate their hard/warning classes or grant scheduling authority. Travel time, location, crew composition, vehicles, equipment, operator qualifications, leave and other shared-resource bottlenecks are **not** checked here. Unknowns in those dimensions may prevent a capacity claim even when person-minutes are calculated.

Part 5B still needs the authenticated reader, compatible workload and resource evidence, target-specific dimension binding, saved-run/outcome joins, and Part 3 evaluation/calibration before `capacity.available_role_hours.v1` can be issued. A reviewed deterministic scenario may eventually use this position as one input, but a current declared schedule is not proof of future attendance or realized production. Part 5C owns advisory bottleneck consequences; Mission 22 keeps schedule and assignment decisions.

`tests/unit/m26-role-time-position-diagnostic.test.js` checks overlap and double-count prevention, horizon clipping, zero versus missing, unknown and cross-tenant rejection, bounds, and an independent minute-by-minute comparison. It does not establish source authenticity, future accuracy, paid/demo behavior, provider evidence or final Part 5B acceptance.
