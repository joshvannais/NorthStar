# Mission 26 Part 5B — guarded declared-availability source

Status: internal, unmounted read-only source prerequisite. This is **not** an issued capacity forecast or final Part 5B acceptance. No route, migration, schedule action, assignment, reservation, or customer-facing value is added.

`readDeclaredAvailabilitySnapshot` reuses Mission 22's organization, current actor/session/subscription/onboarding, and active Business Profile checks inside one repeatable-read transaction. It reads the active tenant roster and the existing canonical declared-availability authorities and intervals through Mission 22's bounded attachment reader. A complete source snapshot requires a current Business Profile hours object, at most 100 active workers, untruncated skill and interval evidence, and declared availability coverage for every active worker across the requested future window. A missing or bounded source returns unavailable, never zero. The snapshot preserves profile, time-zone, interval revision/digest, worker identity, service associations, the database MVCC snapshot identity, a microsecond-precision observation time recorded after the snapshot was established, and one digest for later source-currentness checks. The observation time is an **upper bound on the database read**, not a certified historical as-of cutoff; `temporalCutoffVerified` remains false. It is internal evidence only and carries private workforce information.

The snapshot authenticates **these current source records**, not a worker's qualification for a particular job or exact cutoff eligibility. A workforce operational role or service association is not proof of a certification or safe assignment. The reader does not establish approved commitments, actual leave, travel, crew composition, location reachability, vehicle or equipment availability, operator eligibility, realized attendance, or future capacity. The unmounted role-time diagnostic cannot consume this snapshot as an issued forecast. A later Mission 26 adapter must reconcile the exact role definition, Business Profile working-hour windows and local-time transitions, qualifications, approved M22 commitments, absences, resource constraints and same-cutoff coverage before calculating a constrained capacity position. Any promoted forecast also needs the Part 3 target/output, saved-run/outcome, and evaluation gates. Mission 22 retains assignment authority.

Focused mock-database tests verify that Mission 22's guard is invoked, snapshot fields remain immutable, missing coverage and bounded roster withhold data, an inactive actor is refused, malformed requests never reach storage, and failures roll back. They do not claim disposable-PostgreSQL integration, paid/demo UI, CI, private-production source contents, or final capacity acceptance.
