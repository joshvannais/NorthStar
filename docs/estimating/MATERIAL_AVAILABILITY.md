# Mission24 Part2 Slice6 — Availability And Alternatives

Material plans now carry human-reported availability separately from price evidence. The existing paid and isolated demo review share validation, arithmetic and controls. No supplier integration, physical-stock verification, reservation, order or purchasing authority is introduced.

## Evidence and arithmetic

Each selected material records Unknown, My Observation, Company Record or Supplier Statement, with relevant reference, optional checked/end dates and location, reported quantity in the same explicit unit, and optional calendar-day lead time after ordering. Missing end dates always mean unknown freshness. No automatic conversion or arrival date is invented. Zero available quantity is distinct from missing. Explicit expired-source review retains the expired caution and cannot establish current stock.

The exact waste-inclusive planned quantity comes from the existing calculation. Reported shortage/surplus uses six-decimal scaled arithmetic; only dated, unexpired, located and job-attested evidence permits a current reported-sufficiency label. Reused source identity across separate lines is rejected rather than allocating the same reported pool twice. My Observation without a reference is a line-specific declaration, never company-wide stock. Exact calculation does not verify the declaration.

## Selection and history

Compare An Alternative starts one replacement draft from the current saved v4 plan. Users enter the alternative's quantity, price, evidence, suitability acknowledgement and reason. The saved plan total remains visible beside the proposed total. Cancel does not write. Selection saves one reviewed whole-plan revision and replaces one line rather than adding both costs. Other line changes require a separate save.

A new line records its immediate prior plan/line identity privately. Retained line identities must preserve their immutable replacement provenance during later ordinary edits. Historical plans/adopted estimates retain the original and selected evidence. Adoption remains cost-only, requires current source/decision pins and creates a new estimate needing its own human scope/price decision. Customer pricing, original tax and operational graph are unchanged.

Startup preserves current entry-point-only grants and bounded tighter-of-inherited lock/statement timeouts. No new demo operation or permission is added.

Current owner/admin/session/CSRF checks and authority-before-replay remain. Fresh preview/consent is required after changed evidence, date classifications or revision. Reads compute current cautions separately from immutable saved assessments. Exact replay is historical. A compatible material/adoption pause and combined decision pause must preserve populated v1-v4/alternative history and support reviewed forward resume; old readers are not a rollback lane.

Production release requires a fresh root disposition after independent audit. No production action is part of implementation.
