# Fictional industry registry: first tree-service group

The registry preserves the existing six fictional service definitions and adds tree service with six separate operations: removal, pruning, stump grinding, storm assessment, debris hauling, and an estimate visit. Ordinary Customize, Simulated Lead, reload and reset use the same protected demo paths. The caller-intent choices select a tree operation explicitly; incompatible emergencies are rejected. No tree operation is treated as an equipment assignment, safety clearance, reservation, tax decision or completed work.

New workspaces carry `industry-registry-v1`. Existing workspaces without that marker reproduce their original six-service generator exactly. Their saved data is not upgraded; their selector shows their existing services. A deliberate reset creates a new current workspace. The current generator accepts an unassigned, unscheduled initial assessment only with the corresponding pending statuses. Other identity, territory, contact, digest and source checks remain in place.

The tree pack records taxonomy, authored customer declarations, operation-specific site and disposal questions, source geography/dates, evidence status and review expiration. Its machinery, capacity, workforce credentials, rates, productivity and travel fields explicitly remain unknown. Pruning asks for the appropriate arborist; stump work asks about buried services, access and grinding depth; hauling asks about volume and payload capacity, destination, route legs, driver absence and elapsed time; storm work requires hazard assessment without a response-time promise. An estimate visit does not authorize production work.

The existing canonical calculator is used with a visibly fictional review amount. Tree direct costs, production labor, equipment and travel are **not** filled using the six-service illustrative cost assumptions. The resulting Prepared Estimate remains incomplete. Actual tree records and their transcripts/facts stay attached to the same canonical job across pages. No pricing, tax, workforce or scheduling authority is created by the intelligence pack.

## Review coverage and limits

The firm target remains at least 200 independently reviewed profiles. This first group establishes the reusable registry shape and demonstrates seven available fictional industries; it reports zero profiles accepted against the new comprehensive intelligence-quality contract. The six legacy profiles have not been retrospectively certified. Adding catalog labels does not increase the reviewed count. Random resolution chooses an eligible industry before expanding that industry's options, rather than expanding all 200 profiles at once.

Implemented now: typed operation vocabulary, coherent customer dialogue/scope, distinct uncertainty and safety questions, source-review metadata, new-session versioning, ordinary canonical entry, explicit incomplete costs, isolation/history preservation and cross-page tests. Future reviewed evidence remains necessary for regional measurements/productivity/rates/ranges, specific machinery and payload, eligible workers and current credentials, actual resource feasibility, permits, disposal destinations, taxes and post-job learning. Those facts cannot be inferred from the industry name or synthetic company.

Official OSHA index excerpts support the taxonomy/hazard pointers; full-page acquisition returned 403 and is unavailable. Connecticut's current arborist page was read and its raw bytes pinned. Its scope includes pruning/trimming arboriculture in Connecticut; it does not certify a synthetic worker or provide a universal removal credential rule. The Connecticut landscaping regulation is separately pinned and remains a tax source candidate. Geography and operation applicability must be reviewed independently.

No SQL, dependency, provider configuration, tax source registry, publication worker, legal rule or paid-onboarding allowlist changes are included. Paid onboarding remains independent of this curated demo catalog.
