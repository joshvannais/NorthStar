# Mission 30 — NorthStar Operating System v1.0

## Status

Future mission. Mission 30 is the system-wide composition and v1.0 acceptance mission. It begins only after its upstream operating authorities and their release gates are accepted.

## Product outcome

Deliver one coherent, production-ready NorthStar operating system for service businesses. Mission 30 composes the accepted customer, communication, knowledge, scheduling, execution, estimating, learning, prediction, financial, automation and governance authorities. It removes duplicate projections and disconnected experiences without collapsing those authorities into one mutable record.

## Mission structure

### 1. Cross-System Integration Audit

Verify subsystem cohesion, canonical references, shared projections, revision consistency, error recovery and complete end-to-end workflows. Resolve remaining architecture inconsistencies and prove that every accepted mission works as part of the same product.

### 2. Unified Polaris Experience

Provide one intelligence experience backed by canonical sources and shared engines. Eliminate duplicate calculations, conflicting summaries and disconnected Polaris surfaces. Preserve evidence, confidence, uncertainty, role filtering and explicit action authority.

### 3. Executive Command Center

Deliver the customer-business owner's operational home: morning briefing, current priorities, risks, opportunities, capacity, financial and operational KPIs, forecasts, decision support and direct paths to the underlying work.

### 4. AI Operations Assistant

Allow authorized users to ask natural-language questions about jobs, customers, employees, schedules, assets, finances and forecasts. Answers must resolve canonical records, respect tenant and role scope, cite permitted evidence and distinguish read-only advice from explicitly approved auditable actions.

### 5. End-to-End Automation Validation

Validate owner-controlled workflows across communications, scheduling, operations and finance, including approvals, duplicate requests, retries, provider failures, partial completion, recovery, attribution and audit evidence.

### 6. Enterprise Readiness

Verify security, privacy, performance, scale, availability, tenant isolation, multi-location behavior, roles, delegations, integration boundaries, data lifecycle and operational resilience.

### 7. Launch Readiness

Complete final product consistency, onboarding, documentation, production operations, accessibility, responsive behavior, release evidence and the v1.0 acceptance checklist. Automated validation does not replace the founder's separate visual verdict.

## Authority boundaries

- Mission 30 composes accepted authorities; it does not silently replace their source records or ownership.
- Mission 31 owns the deeper isolated living-business simulation.
- Mission 32 owns the contractor-facing On-the-Fly Calculator.
- Mission 33 owns the private NorthStar platform administration and company-intelligence control plane.
- Provider activation, legal approval and private-production evidence remain unavailable until actually supplied and accepted.

## Acceptance gates

- Complete cross-mission requirement-to-source, requirement-to-interface and requirement-to-evidence ledgers
- Mounted production modules with durable PostgreSQL authority and exact tenant/role isolation
- End-to-end desktop, tablet and mobile validation in Chrome and actual Playwright WebKit
- Loading, empty, error, retry, offline boundary and recovery validation
- Performance, security, privacy, accessibility and enterprise-readiness evidence
- Independent immutable release audit with zero unresolved P0–P3 findings
- Normal merge, exact automatic deployment, health, bounded passive production acceptance and final-ref seal
- Separate founder visual approval after the final deployed experience is available
