# Mission 27 Part 3C — billable-work and Polaris quote review

Status: implementation candidate. This record defines original Part 3C and additive Q6 only. It does not accept the candidate, begin Part 3D, authorize an invoice, deliver a proposal, create work, or claim that external signature or receiving adapters exist.

## Explicit billable-work review

`GET` and `POST /api/v1/customer-financial/editable-estimates/:estimateId/billable-work-review` let a current paid-tenant owner or administrator select one source class, inspect its exact source evidence, and record why that source is invoiceable. A final balance requires the exact current Part 3A Mission 24 commercial basis and current Part 3B Mission 23 completion and handoff evidence. A pre-work deposit requires one exact accepted Mission 24 deposit schedule and satisfies the frozen amount and percentage bounds without claiming completion. Milestones remain value-free as `milestone_authority_unsupported` until Mission 23 supplies separately accepted milestone or work-package authority.

The database resolves current membership, subscription, recent authentication, CSRF, the action-specific `customer_financial.billable_work.review` capability, frozen `mission27.launch.v1` policy, source class, source identity, source and authority digests, amount, and control mode. The effect accepts only a source-class choice and the digest the browser reviewed. Inside the guarded database boundary it reconstructs the exact-current Mission 24 commercial lineage and, for a final balance, the current Mission 23 completion plus prepared handoff. Caller-authored amounts, source objects, work identities and authority digests are rejected and cannot become durable evidence. An exact amount through USD 10,000 uses single control; a larger supported amount through USD 100,000 requires two distinct authorized reviewers. The source decision remains nonfinancial: it grants no invoice, provider, receiver, customer-contact, scheduling, work, or learning action.

The accepted review is append-only and tenant-private. Exact request replay is bound to the actor, session, request key, source decision and grant revision. A changed request conflicts. Commercial and per-work database fences require a current source re-read before the review commits; reopened work, changed commercial evidence, entitlement loss, superseded authorization, or a different source digest clears older value. Exact zero remains a supported amount and is distinct from unavailable evidence.

## Shared mobile Polaris quote review

The mounted customer detail loads `m27-polaris-quote-journey-v1` for paid, authenticated owners, administrators and currently authorized members. It projects the current Part 3A Mission 24 basis into one editable view over the same estimate and source identities. Every option name, scope, validity period, assumption, line description, quantity, unit, and exact USD price is editable. Users may add or remove ordered lines and up to three options within the frozen limits. The browser performs no floating-point money arithmetic; exact minor units and deterministic server digests bind every save. Members can receive only the frozen member-grantable edit-draft and edit-options capabilities; signature and work-handoff capabilities remain owner/admin controlled and cannot be minted or used by a member.

Draft saves require the exact current edit capability, optimistic draft revision, and pins to the exact-current source-owned Mission 24 issued customer-estimate version. Its source proposal digest, revision digest, reviewed recipient and accepted-delivery evidence are read from Mission 24 records inside the database boundary. A Mission 27 draft never creates, relabels or approves a parallel Mission 24 proposal. A change invalidates signature and handoff states. The current draft table deliberately does not claim Part 3D's immutable original-versus-human revision history; Part 3D remains responsible for that history and complete invalidation lifecycle.

The view displays the source-owned current Mission 24 issued proposal reference, exact document digest and reviewed recipient. Part 3C does not expose a Mission 27 proposal-review endpoint or use `customer_financial.estimate.request_signature` to authorize an internal substitute. Automatic delivery remains false.

External typed-name signature remains named and value-free as `legal_review_unavailable`; professional legal review is still required before external signature launch. Accepted-estimate-to-work remains named and value-free as `work_handoff_receiver_unavailable` because `mission23.work_intake.review.v1` has no accepted receiving adapter. The journey creates no appointment, assignment, reservation, dispatch, execution, invoice, payment, provider request, receiver mutation, or learning write. Mission 24 retains estimate, price, proposal, option, signature and acceptance authority; Mission 22 scheduling and dispatch; Mission 23 work intake, execution and evidence; Mission 25 tenant-private learning; Mission 32 the later deep calculator over the shared canonical model.

The editor is keyboard operable, announces loading/success/failure, uses explicit labels and stable focus targets, and inherits responsive light/dark styles. Each load has a same-mount generation token, so an older delayed response cannot replace a newer response. Every mutation rejection clears draft values, prices, grants and action controls before offering an explicit reload. It is loaded on the shared paid shells but refuses the account-free demo surface. The isolated fictional demo counterpart remains a later Mission 27 acceptance slice.
