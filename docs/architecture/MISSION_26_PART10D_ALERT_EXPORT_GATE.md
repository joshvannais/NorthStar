## Alert and next-move contract

An issued alert needs a versioned trigger policy, exact target and reporting window, accepted run/revision, source-currentness decision, applicable baseline or threshold, materiality, direction, uncertainty, explanation and owner-visible time. A threshold crossing caused by stale, revoked, corrected or incomplete evidence becomes unavailable or requires review, never a confident alert. Stable deduplication requires the exact run, policy and event identities and must distinguish a changed run from a repeated page load. An alert's label must say whether it is a deterministic scenario, calibrated forecast or observed condition. No percentage likelihood appears without Part 3C/9 calibration. The mounted contract holds all alert values, policy/event/deduplication identities and digests null until those prerequisites exist.

## Export boundary

An export is a **point-in-time evidence packet**, not a promise that old advice remains current. It must identify the tenant, authorized viewer and export time; run/revision and output digests; target/version, unit and horizon; reporting-window and business-calendar basis; source snapshot/as-of and coverage; algorithm/calculation and scenario or interval basis; uncertainty; alert-policy version and reason; current correction/invalidation state; and explicit missing evidence. It must separate supplied prediction, later authenticated actual, calculated difference and human action. It excludes raw transcripts, customer contact details, wages, secrets and unrelated records. Part 1C already owns permission, source-validity recheck, private/no-store response, safe audit and paid/demo isolation; Part 10D consumes those controls instead of duplicating them. The mounted boundary returns no packet or URL. The fictional demo makes no paid route call and cannot contain paid tenant evidence.

Export of a superseded run may be allowed only through separately authorized historical access with a visible superseded/stale label and lawful retention. Revocation or deletion can remove current access to source-derived detail without rewriting an immutable run receipt. A link, download or share target must not be exposed before the route can enforce those boundaries. Mission 28 owns any later approved outbound communication; this slice does not send alerts to customers or staff.
