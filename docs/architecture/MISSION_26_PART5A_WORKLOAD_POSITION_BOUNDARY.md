# Mission 26 Part 5A — workload and base-capacity research boundary

Status: target-complete implementation candidate awaiting independent exact-head audit and release. Canonical acceptance remains Open-partial until the separate post-release refresh finishes.

Part 5A now implements three registered, tenant-private 30-elapsed-day research targets. `workload.accepted_person_hours.v1` is an event flow of accepted, closed, non-overlapping Mission 23 labor intervals. `workload.end_backlog_hours.v1` is an exclusive-horizon-end stock of owner-reviewed remaining person-hours, with scheduled and unscheduled totals kept distinct. `capacity.available_role_hours.v1` is base role capacity from an exact reviewed role qualification and declared Mission 22 availability, less approved commitments. All arithmetic uses integer person-minutes and persists private values; paid responses expose identities, currentness, horizons and recovery state while withholding results, output digests and evaluation metrics.

## Prospective source authority

Coverage starts at an explicit append-only epoch after source locks drain. The installed authority pins bounded NorthStar records only: approved Mission 23 work profiles and reviewed certification identities, Mission 22 declared availability revisions and intervals, approved schedule assignments and commitments, accepted Mission 23 labor and completion evidence, and approved Mission 24 person-plan reviews. The backlog path first enumerates the complete accepted, non-cancelled booking population and only then enriches each unfinished identity with exactly one current Mission 24 plan and remaining-hours review. Scheduled, explicitly unscheduled and reopened unfinished work therefore cannot disappear because its plan is absent. Exact decision, labor-plan and source-generation identities are revalidated under the owning M24 fences; a correction or withdrawal makes the old evidence stale, while ordinary later schedule or completion progress remains a future outcome rather than a rewrite of the cutoff.

The applicable workforce population is also observed append-only from the prospective epoch. Profile, membership and account writers append the exact source order, source digest, account and membership state, and declared operational role. Ended-window capacity reconstructs the last visible roster observation at the window start instead of consulting the latest mutable roster. A later suspension, reactivation or role change therefore cannot remove a worker from an already-ended result, even when the visible value later returns to its prior value. NorthStar does not yet have an authority for retroactively effective workforce membership or role corrections. This slice refuses to invent or backfill one: supported changes are source-observed from their commit forward, while any requested effective-dated historical rewrite remains unavailable and must not create a partial receipt.

Base capacity has a separate append-only role-scope decision. It fixes one declared operational role for the research target, and the origin, outcome and evaluation carry that exact role. The authority enumerates every active workforce profile that Mission 22 can assign before selecting the requested role. Every profile needs a current Part 5A qualification decision bound to its source-owned operational role, account and membership plus a monotonic per-profile source generation. A profile, membership or account change advances that generation, so restoring the prior visible value cannot revive an older decision or exact-key replay. For member profiles, the decision additionally requires the accepted Mission 23 approved work profile and reviewed certification identity. Owner, admin and viewer profiles cannot submit that Mission 23 self-profile; their narrower decision therefore records only an owner-reviewed internal operational-role basis and explicitly does not claim independent credentials or provider certification. It can contribute capacity only after that explicit decision and a separate current declared-availability review; absence of an M23 self-profile is never treated as satisfied qualification. This keeps potentially assignable people in the census without fabricating credentials. Every selected worker also needs a current Mission 22 availability review; missing or conflicting evidence refuses the whole capture. A role with no members is authenticated zero only after that whole active assignable census has current classification authority. Distinct roles are never pooled or silently substituted. An exact approved assigned commitment is subtracted from base capacity when Mission 22 has no review state or retains only `location_scope_authority_missing` and `required_skill_authority_missing`, the job-specific constraint authorities owned by Part 5B. Every other review reason, non-object or empty review state still refuses Part 5A. This conservative classification cannot turn unresolved later constraints into free capacity and does not weaken the Part 5A role-qualification, availability, population, interval or source requirements. Missing, ambiguous, conflicting, oversized or unreviewed evidence refuses the whole capture. Complete installed-source zero is distinct from missing evidence. Provider, off-platform, legacy, unsaved and whole-business coverage remain unavailable.

An owner or admin must separately approve the exact method for each target. Role-scope, role-qualification, declared-availability-basis and remaining-work reviews are append-only, source-bound decisions with expected revision and digest checks. `GET /api/v1/forecast/workload-capacity/reviews/current` returns only the exact current review token and whether its source remains current, so a lost response or stale replay can be recovered through a fresh explicit decision without direct database access; it withholds source payloads, members, hours and private forecast data. A reviewed qualification confirms only the bounded internal role basis and cited accepted profile/certification record; it is not an independent license, provider certification or realized attendance claim. A remaining-hours review is bound to the exact approved assignment and Mission 24 person plan; it does not approve completion or replace Mission 23 completion authority.

Mission 22's accepted human-preview route can schedule, reschedule, dispatch and unassign, but it cannot preserve assignment identity while returning scheduled accepted work to the unscheduled queue. Part 5A therefore adds one narrow explicit owner/admin action at `POST /api/v1/forecast/workload-capacity/backlog-items/:id/unschedule`. The guarded entry requires the exact current assignment revision/digest, CSRF, reason, confirmation and idempotency identity; it writes the existing Mission 22 schedule approval, assignment revision, audit and idempotency authorities atomically. It never changes the approved work target, appointment status, estimate, execution or commercial state, and it never acts automatically.

The source fence covers every participating source table and writer transaction. Captures acquire the released supporting-authority fence, paid-access rows, source tables and tenant fence in one deterministic order. The initial authorization locks remain held through commit; post-wait and replay paths reread the exact actor, session, subscription and CSRF state without requesting the same locks again, preventing a queued revocation from causing a lock-queue inversion. Every receipt persists atomically. The additive migration also corrects three older deferred integrity functions so legitimate runtime field-execution, labor and completion writes can finish COMMIT while their underlying tables and private helpers remain denied to the runtime role. It changes those validators to safe-search-path security-definer integrity checks; it does not change their owning write contracts, evidence chronology or immutable guards.

## Windows, origins and outcomes

Every finalized window is exactly 2,592,000 elapsed seconds and half-open. Canonical occurrence at the exclusive end belongs to the next window. The accepted-person-hours method uses the mean of two complete preceding windows. Backlog and base-capacity inputs are reconstructed at the immutable prediction cutoff. The explicit owner/admin origin action authorizes the server to finalize exactly the two contiguous preceding windows and save the origin in one transaction. After all blocking source and access locks drain and are rechecked, the server issues the cutoff from its own clock, finalizes both receipts, and starts a fully future 2,592,000-second horizon; a refusal rolls back both windows and the origin. Exact-key replay returns only the unchanged current receipt, while changed human authorization requires a new key. The future origin fixes ordered target and method identities, exact training-window receipts, reviewed source identities and a private output digest. A later explicit coverage epoch permanently retires every window and origin pinned to an older epoch, even when no source value changed. Currentness checks reject the older epoch before recomputing evidence, so a newer source-order baseline can never erase an intervening transition or revive an old receipt. Evaluations inherit both the origin and outcome-window epoch checks. Recovery requires a new human-reviewed epoch, a complete new prospective history period, and a new origin; an epoch never backfills or rewrites older history. Callers cannot select clocks, cutoffs, horizons, members, counts, source manifests, forecasts or actuals.

Evaluation happens only after the horizon and requires a separate current finalized outcome for the identical half-open interval. Its workload flow, horizon-end backlog stock and same-target base-capacity result are pinned without rewriting the origin. Capacity is measured only when the exact role qualification, active workforce, declared-availability basis and approved commitment population apply throughout the interval, rather than treating everyone active at the start as qualified and available for all later instants. The installed sources have prospective observation order but no authority for retroactively effective workforce or availability intervals. An account, membership, operational-role, Mission 23 profile or reviewed-certification generation, capacity-review, declared-availability, crew-membership or approved-commitment observation strictly inside a window therefore makes that capacity outcome unavailable and persists no receipt; the slice refuses to ignore the change or invent its effective time. The Mission 23 generation ledger begins at the explicit installed-source epoch: an already-approved profile receives one prospective baseline, and every later submit, reject, revoke, replacement or reapproval is observed append-only under the owning writer fence. Restoring the same visible profile or certification never revives an older generation or replay. A change exactly at the exclusive end belongs to the next interval, including a newly approved commitment whose decision time equals that boundary. Changes observed after that boundary cannot rewrite a supported ended result. An outcome recapture after those later changes uses the same historical roster and role-specific minutes, creates a new immutable outcome revision, makes the prior evaluation stale, and requires append-only reevaluation. Ordinary completion or reopening after the measured boundary is also future evolution. A later supported correction to a pre-boundary completion or other source fact changes the affected evidence and makes the older receipt stale. Test-only source clocks timestamp newly inserted completion decisions, Mission 22 schedule approvals and observations, and guarded Mission 24 person-plan reviews in disposable databases so chronological origin-before-outcome evidence can be exercised. Declared-availability rows retain Mission 22's own immutable timestamps and idempotency contract; the Part 5A source fence separately appends their visibility time to a private observation ledger. Production uses the real transaction clock, prior immutable rows are never changed, and all clock and observation authorities are denied to runtime and PUBLIC.

## Security and product limits

Writes require a current paid owner/admin session, CSRF, bounded idempotency key and expected source or review revision/digest where applicable. Reads and writes recheck current access after source locks; the origin action also rechecks after its exact-key wait before issuing the server cutoff. Missing and cross-tenant identities return the same generic result. Private tables, sequences, clocks, access rechecks, projections and calculation helpers are denied to PUBLIC and runtime; runtime receives only guarded entry functions. Startup verifies every guarded entry; every critical clock, fence, access, evidence, projection and currentness helper; exact enabled immutability and source-fence triggers; all disposable chronology triggers; and safe security-definer search paths. It fails closed if any function becomes invoker-rights, gains an unsafe path, disappears, or is attached to the wrong or disabled trigger. Mounted evidence uses the genuine Mission 23 record-and-accept writer for 1,001 source intervals in the second server-selected training window and proves a bounded origin action returns `413` while rolling back the first training receipt, the failing receipt and the origin rather than truncating or partially persisting the complete population.

Apart from the explicit human-controlled return to the existing Mission 22 unscheduled state described above, this slice performs no scheduling, assignment, dispatch, estimate, employment, hiring or commercial action. It does not establish job-specific crew/skill/location/travel/vehicle/equipment-constrained capacity; Part 5B owns that composition. It issues no public or paid numeric forecast and claims no natural production history, empirical accuracy, calibration, drift result, statistical confidence, legal/provider conclusion, WebKit or physical-device coverage, complete accessibility or founder visual verdict.
