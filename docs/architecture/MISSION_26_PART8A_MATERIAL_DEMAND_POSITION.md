# Mission 26 Part 8A — material demand prerequisite

The unmounted `src/forecasting/materialDemandPositionDiagnostic.js` is a bounded, deterministic quantity-position diagnostic for caller-supplied rows. It retains the pinned estimate identity, revision, digest, line identity, recorded time, required time, specification, procurement location and unit. It sums only matching specifications, locations, units and exact required instants with six-decimal fixed-point arithmetic. Different units are never converted or pooled. Incomplete, revoked, truncated or out-of-window claimed coverage withholds every quantity. Duplicate estimate-line identities, cross-tenant rows, future records, fractional item counts and malformed input are rejected.

This diagnostic does not calculate waste, source a plan or claim that a row is approved. Its `plannedQuantity` must eventually come from the sealed Mission 24 material calculator and an authenticated current adopted-plan reader; the caller's `sourceSnapshotDigest` and coverage are not authenticated here. Mission 23 material movements record job execution/usage, not a complete on-hand inventory ledger. Mission 24 V4 availability is a dated statement about a reviewed job, not reserved stock, future receipts or a purchasing commitment. Consequently `sourceAuthenticated`, `approvedPlanVerified`, `inventoryVerified`, `receiptsVerified` and `forecastIssued` always remain false; reorder date, stockout risk and purchasing risk remain unavailable. A claimed complete empty input describes only the hypothetical submitted set, not proven zero demand.

Final Part 8A acceptance requires a tenant/role-guarded, complete as-of source adapter for currently approved jobs and material revisions, explicit material/stock identities and compatible units, source-owned on-hand balances and transaction/receipt semantics (or an unavailable state), replenishment lead times and cutoff policies, change/revocation propagation, supplier availability and purchase authority where applicable, real outcome evaluation, paid/demo recovery and independent end-to-end audit. This prerequisite adds no route, migration, inventory authority, automatic order or customer-visible forecast. It must not be read as Part 8A acceptance.
