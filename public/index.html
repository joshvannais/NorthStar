One Operating View for Contractor Work

NorthStar connects the operational context around calls, customers, work, schedules, estimates, and outcomes.

Calls and Lead Context

When a workspace has an authorized call provider, captured conversations can become tenant-scoped leads and follow-up context.

Customer and Work Records

Keep meaningful customer, lead, and work records connected to the people who are authorized to act on them.

Explainable Polaris Estimates

Polaris keeps estimate inputs, confidence, missing information, and recommended next steps visible instead of hiding the reasoning.

Schedules and Attention

See appointments, conflicts, and work that needs attention without claiming an action happened before an authorized person confirms it.

Operational Next Actions

Turn captured context into clear, role-aware next steps across the Contractor Command Center and mounted work surfaces.

Outcome Learning

Use outcome learning to tie completed work and business results back to the original context so recommendations can improve without crossing tenant boundaries.

How It Works

Three truthful stages connect incoming context to accountable work.

1

Capture Authorized Context

Calls and other connected inputs enter a tenant-scoped workflow only when the workspace and integration are configured for them.

2

Organize and Recommend

NorthStar connects the customer, lead, work, estimate, schedule, and next-action context that authorized team members need.

3

Act, Record, and Learn

People remain responsible for consequential actions; completed work and outcomes return to the operating record for future learning.
